## OUT

- Amendments after the book (103rd-106th) and recent judgements: not in the text; none asserted.
- Ordinary-bill procedure beyond the contrast in p.196.
- Table 12.1 case detail beyond element names.

## Falsifier decisions
- 'Three-fourths of the states' appears only as the US figure (p.196), and as a false claim about India; 'all the states' and 'prior recommendation of the President' are false in real law and in the chapter.
- Counts ("eighteen" simple-majority matters, "eight" federal matters, "twenty" basic-structure elements, "seven" criticisms, nine/six/three table elements) are always worded "the chapter lists".
- The 42nd-for-24th swap and 103rd-for-101st swap are used as falsifiers: both numbers are real amendments, so plausible, and false.
- Table 12.1 falsifiers (Raghunath Rao, Indira Gandhi federal character, Effective access to justice for Kesavananda) are worded "according to Table 12.1" and are false of those cases in real law too.
- Footnote-only claims: Achary quotation (Lok Sabha Rules at all effective stages) and the Wheare Modern Constitutions (1966) citation are phrased "according to the chapter".

## Known looseness
Concept LK-11.2 shares the Article 13 case chain with LK-11.5 (Kesavananda); each concept keeps a given idea to two items, but a few ideas appear a third time as one statement inside a match or multi-statement item.
