## OUT of scope (not on the pages, nothing from memory)
- The brief's items on expenditure limits, anti-defection links, the 2023 CEC/EC appointment law, one nation one election, ADR, PUCL and the Lily Thomas ordinance episode: the chapter does not carry them, so no question uses them.
- Electoral bonds: only the 2018 scheme as the chapter describes it; stems say "as described in the chapter"; no statement on later status (struck down in Feb 2024 is NOT on the page and is not asserted either way).
- Any "first use of EVMs" claim (chapter's 1998 and 1999 dates describe experimental/Goa use; real first use was earlier, so not asserted).
- Tagged PYQ 1997 on countries funding elections (Germany/Austria) is not in the chapter; not used.

## Facts least certain
1. Death of a candidate (p.827): the chapter says no countermanding but a recognised party may propose a replacement in seven days; real s.52 practice is more nuanced, so items are worded "according to the chapter" and no falsifier relies on the countermanding rule itself (the false statements are 30 days and a "whichever is more" style number).
2. Photographs on EVMs after 1 May 2015 (p.832): the date comes from the chapter alone.
3. Open ballot (p.829) applies to party electors in Rajya Sabha polls only; falsifier says it extends to legislative councils.
4. Overseas voters (p.830): the chapter lists employment, education "or otherwise"; the falsifier "only employment" relies on that list.
5. Electoral bond buyer-information confidentiality (p.832): falsifier says it is "regularly disclosed to the Election Commission"; the chapter says it is confidential except to courts or in criminal cases.
