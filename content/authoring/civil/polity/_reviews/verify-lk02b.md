## Structural weaknesses of the old set (all concepts)
- All 120 items are single-line direct recall, Remember/Easy; none is a "which statements", "how many", Statement-I/II or match item as UPSC has set since 2020.
- Items 15-20 of each concept are templated reasoning stems ("Why is...", "Which chart...") with obviously absurd distractors.
- Several stems are not self-contained ("Choose the correct option. Article ____ ...").
- LK-2.7 #3 "Which party won a single seat? Communist Party" is true but not unique in meaning (seven parties won one seat).
