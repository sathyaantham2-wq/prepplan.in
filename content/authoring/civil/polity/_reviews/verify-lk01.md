## Structural weaknesses of the old set
- All 120 items are single-line recall, Remember/Easy; none is a "which statements", "how many", Statement-I/II or match item as UPSC has set them since 2020.
- Items 15-20 of every concept are templated "How/Why/Which chart" stems with obviously absurd distractors.
- Several stems are not self-contained ("Choose the correct option. ...").
