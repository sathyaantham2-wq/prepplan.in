## Structural weaknesses of the old set (all concepts)
- All 120 items are single-line direct recall, Remember/Easy; none is a "which statements", "how many", Statement-I/II or match item as UPSC used since 2020.
- Items 15-20 of every concept are templated reasoning stems ("Why is...", "Which chart...", "Which poster idea...") with obviously absurd distractors (e.g. "tax, toll, trade, trouble").
- Several stems are not self-contained ("Choose the correct option. Article ____ ...").

## New pack
content/authoring/civil/polity/packs/lk05.json: same concept codes LK-5.1 to LK-5.6, 20 questions each (5 direct, 9 which-statements, 3 how-many, 2 Statement-I/II, 1 match), recall at most 4 per concept, every fact cited to pdf pages 85-92. Validator: 0 problems.
