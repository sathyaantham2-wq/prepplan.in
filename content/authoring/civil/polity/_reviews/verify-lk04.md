## Structural weaknesses of the old set (all concepts)
- All 120 items are single-line recall, Remember/Easy; no "which statements", "how many", Statement-I/II or match items as UPSC has used since 2020.
- Items 15-20 of each concept are templated reasoning stems ("Why is...", "Which chart...") with obviously absurd distractors.
- Several stems are not self-contained ("Choose the correct option. ... ____").
- Duplicated ideas: LK-4.6 #5 and #20 test the same reply; LK-4.2 #2/#3 are two lookups of the same scholar list.
