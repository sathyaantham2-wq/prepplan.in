## Chapter wording followed deliberately (not independently verified)
- Speaker may vote in the first instance during his own removal resolution but not in a tie (p.336), as Article 96.
- National emergency approval is listed under special majority-I (p.349); Lok Sabha resolution disapproving emergency under simple majority.
- "Cannot be challenged in a court" for dissolution (p.331) was not used as a falsifier.
- Detention of a pre-1979 date for the 44th Amendment is given as "20 June 1979" in the text (p.379); not asked.
- Chapter says Speaker is placed seventh with the CJI and above all cabinet ministers except PM/Deputy PM (p.338).
- Table 23.5 verified by summation: RS 225+8+12=245, LS 524+19=543, SC 84, ST 47.
- Footnotes 20-22 (joint sittings) are on p.392, footnote 5 on pp.390-391, footnote 13 on p.391.
