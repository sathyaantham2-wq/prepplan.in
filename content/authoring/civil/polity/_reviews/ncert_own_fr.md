## Old-file audit claim: POL-1.2 #13
Confirmed. Old stem: "A citizen is offered a title by a foreign government. Under Article 18 he"; key "may not accept it without the consent of the President". Art 18(2) bars a citizen outright; the President's-consent clause for titles (Art 18(3)) applies only to a NON-citizen holding an office of profit or trust, and clause (4) (presents, emoluments, office) applies to any office-holder. So the old key is wrong for a citizen (it implies acceptance with consent is possible). The old key is not right "for a non-citizen office holder" because the stem says citizen. Minor imprecision in the verify note: it says "the consent clause applies to holders of an office of profit or trust"; precisely, for titles it applies to non-citizen office-holders only (ch8 p. item 3), for presents/emoluments to citizens and non-citizens alike.
