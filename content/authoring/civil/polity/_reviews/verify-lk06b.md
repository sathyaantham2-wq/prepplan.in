## Chapter-internal inconsistencies avoided in the new pack
- Himachal Pradesh is called a "new state" by the Table 6.4 entry of 1954 (item 3) but is a union territory in Table 6.2 (1956) and is "elevated to a state" in 1971; the pack uses only the 1956 and 1971 positions.
- Telangana is "29th state" (p.101) while the list shows 28 states (p.103, J&K now a UT); the pack states both only as the chapter words them and never asks for a current count of states created.
- 1971/1972 (North-Eastern Areas Act vs effect), 1962/1963 (Nagaland), 1970/1971 (Himachal Act): the pack asks only for the year the text itself gives (1972, 1963, 1971) and offers no distractor that is the other defensible year.
- "Part B had nine states" and "Table 6.1 lists nine": consistent. The J&K entry in Part B and "Kashmir" among the three refusing states are treated as the same unit.
