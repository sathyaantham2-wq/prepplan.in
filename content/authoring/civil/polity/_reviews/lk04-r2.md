## HIGH
**H1 - CS-POL-ch04-506** (not in the changed list). Stem: "A state emergency (President's Rule) can be proclaimed on the ground of failure to comply with the directions of the Centre. Under which Article?" Key: Article 365. Option "Article 356" is also defensible. The stem itself calls it "President's Rule", and the proclamation is always made under Art 356. Art 365 only supplies the deeming ground. The chapter attaches 365 to the ground (p.69, Table 4.2 p.78), but the stem asks "under which Article" the emergency is proclaimed, and an aspirant who knows the Constitution would pick 356. Two defensible answers.
Fix: reword the stem to "According to the chapter, which Article is linked with the ground of failure to comply with the directions of the Centre?" or drop 356 from the options and ask "which Article deals with the effect of failure to comply with directions given by the Union".
