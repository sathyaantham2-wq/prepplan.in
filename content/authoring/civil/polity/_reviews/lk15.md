## Author's least-sure facts, tested
1. **Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram: the President, not the Governor (p.222): TRUE, in the book and in law.** p.222 says "The President enjoys the
   same power with respect to tribal areas (autonomous districts) in Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram." Sixth Schedule paras 12A (Meghalaya),
   12AA (Tripura) and 12B (Mizoram) give the Act-of-Parliament direction to the President, while para 12 (Assam) gives it to the Governor. The
   falsifier (115 st.4, "Governor of Mizoram may direct that an Act of Parliament not apply") is false on both counts. Only the Act-of-Parliament
   power is in the statement, so the Governor's separate power over state Acts does not make it defensible. 115 and 116 are fine.
2. **Art 258 vs 258A: direction is RIGHT.** Table 15.2 (pp.245-246): 258 = "Power of the Union to confer powers, etc., on states"; 258A = "Power of the
   states to entrust functions to the Union"; footnote 6 (p.249): the states' power was added by the 7th Amendment (1956). This matches the real
   Constitution. 315 st.4 (258 described as the states' power) is a clean swap-falsifier; 317 st.2 is true.
3. **Basic customs duty not subsumed in GST: TRUE in law** (Entry 83 of the Union List stays; only CVD/ACD and SAD were subsumed). But p.231
   proves it only by omission. See L1 (510).
4. **Punchhi order of preference (pp.241-242): correct as keyed.** (a) pre-poll alliance with the largest number, (b) largest single party, (c)
   post-poll coalition with all partners in government, (d) post-poll alliance with some outside support. 810 st.3 ("largest single party always
   first even if a pre-poll alliance has more") is false against item (iii)(a)-(b). Only one item uses it (good).
5. **Profession tax Rs 250 to Rs 2,500, 60th Amendment 1988: TRUE** (fn 11 p.249; Art 276(2)). 504 st.2 reverses the direction and is a fair
   swap. Punchhi's "do away with the ceiling" (811 st.3, p.243) is also correct.
6. **Currency.** No item rests on GST compensation, GST rates, the 15th/16th Finance Commission percentages or any post-2022 change. Seventh Schedule
   counts (98 / 59 / 52; footnotes 1-3) check arithmetically: 97 - {33, 92} + {2A, 92A, 92B} = 98; 66 - 7 omitted = 59; 47 + 5 = 52. Ladakh as an
   Art 240 UT is current. 80th Amendment 29% and "retrospectively from 1 April 1996" are correct. Nothing is outdated except the points in L5/L6.

## HIGH

**H1 - CS-POL-ch15-717 (howmany, LK-15.7): statement 4 is false only because the book's list is silent, and it is arguably true in current life.**
The statement "Delimitation of Lok Sabha constituencies among the states" is keyed false because "delimitation is not among the 14 tension areas
listed" (p.237). The stem says "the issues that created tensions between the Centre and the states", not "according to Laxmikanth's list of 14".
Delimitation and the freeze on seat shares is, in fact, a live Centre-state/north-south dispute, so a well-read student can defend "All four"
against the key "Only three". This is exactly the recurring defect (false by silence). Fix: hedge the stem ("Laxmikanth lists fourteen tension
areas ...") and keep the delimitation statement, or replace it with a swap such as "Reservation of Central bills for the consideration of the
President" (which 709 st.1 already uses, so choose a different one, for example "Appointment of the Comptroller and Auditor-General by the
states").

**H2 - LK-15.8 `idea` is unsupported for the Punchhi Commission (stored in the concept row).**
"Both commissions favoured cooperative arrangements within a strong Centre rather than structural change." The page supports this only for
Sarkaria (p.238: "did not favour structural changes", "strong Centre essential", p.239). For Punchhi p.240-243 says the opposite on structure: it
asks for the Union to be "extremely restrained in asserting Parliamentary supremacy" (rec 2), for amendments to Art 263 (rec 20), for equality of
Rajya Sabha seats (rec 25), a constitutional definition of local-body devolution (rec 26), a ceiling-free profession tax by constitutional
amendment (rec 29) and a "localised emergency" framework (rec 19). It never says "strong Centre". Rewrite, for example: "Sarkaria kept the
structure and stressed a strong Centre and functional change; Punchhi made cooperative federalism the key and sought Union restraint."
