## The author's five least-sure facts, tested

   socialistic (living wage) and Gandhian (cottage industries); Art 48 Gandhian (cattle) and liberal-intellectual (agriculture).
   In the real Constitution each is a single Article containing both limbs, so no real-law contradiction. Every item that uses the
   dual listing is stemmed "according to / classed by Laxmikanth" (948, 950, 958). 950: statement 2 ("48 only Gandhian") and 4
   ("44 Gandhian") are false on the book and on the real text; statements 1 and 3 true. Key "1 and 3 only" is sound. The
   2012 PYQ (UPSC-POL-2012-213) treats the "leisure and cultural opportunities" limb of Art 43 as not Gandhian, which is consistent
   (it is the living-wage limb); no conflict. OK.
2. **Chagla: not misleading.** 933 statement 1 says "a former Chief Justice" (true in real life, Bombay High Court, and true per
   the book). Statement 3 swaps Setalvad to "Chief Justice of India" (false: Attorney General). A reader cannot be pushed to a wrong
   key by the missing "of India". LOW only (L1).
3. **Art 39 letters: correct against the real text.** (a) livelihood, (b) distribution of ownership and control, (c) concentration,
   (d) equal pay, (e) health and strength of workers / tender age of children, (f) opportunities for healthy development of children
   plus protection of childhood and youth. 953 (key 1, 3, 4) is right on both book and Constitution. 971 statement 3 ("whole of (f)
   inserted by the 42nd") is false in real law too: the original (f) already protected childhood and youth against exploitation;
   the 42nd added only the "opportunities and facilities to develop in a healthy manner" limb. OK.
4. **Art 31C falsifier (988 statement 1, "Articles 14, 19 or 21"): false in real law and per the chapter.** The 25th Amendment text
   names Articles 14, 19 and 31. Key "2 and 4 only" is sound; 3 ("originally any Directive Principle") is false (originally only 39(b),(c)).
5. **Art 44 "only Union Territories" (959) and the Setalvad / Austin office swaps (933): sound.** Art 44 reads "throughout the
   territory of India" and the book says "throughout the country". Setalvad was Attorney General; Austin was American. Fair falsifiers.

relies on a date a UPSC candidate would consider wrong. Fix: drop the year from statement 3 ("Nationalisation of general
insurance") or replace statement 3 with another measure in the same para (p.182 item 5); keep the key unique.
