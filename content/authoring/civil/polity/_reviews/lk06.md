## HIGH
**H1 - CS-POL-ch06-418 (LK-6.4, Part C states, Statement-I/II): the relation is arguable, so two answers are defensible.**
I: Part C states were centrally administered. II: Part C states consisted of erstwhile chief commissioner's provinces and some
princely states. The key says "both correct, II does not explain I". But chief commissioner's provinces were, by their nature,
centrally administered, so II is a fair reason for I and "II explains I" (option 1) is also defensible. The explanation
("composition is a separate fact") is an opinion; the chapter (p.96) gives the two facts side by side with no causal link.
Fix: make II an unambiguously unrelated true fact (for example "Part C states numbered ten", or "the Andaman and Nicobar Islands
were the only Part D territory"), or turn it into a clear cause/effect pair.

**M3 - CS-POL-ch06-114 (LK-6.1): "At present India has 29 states and 7 union territories, according to Laxmikanth."** The key
(false) is right: p.103 and Table 6.3 say 28 and 8. But the stem names the author, which is a reading device, and the
chapter itself calls Telangana the "29th state" (p.101, p.107), so a student who recalls the ordinal sees a false
statement that the book half-supports. Reword to "At present India has 28 states and 8 union territories" with the false
variant "29 states and 7 union territories", dropping "according to Laxmikanth". No item rests on the Telangana ordinal
(author note 1 confirmed; Telangana appears only in 612 as "came into existence in 2014").

**M4 - CS-POL-ch06-113 (LK-6.1), statement 4: "The Sixth Schedule contains the provisions for the administration of scheduled
areas."** It is false (Fifth = scheduled areas, Sixth = tribal areas), but the cited p.93 only says "the Fifth and Sixth
Schedules contain separate provisions with respect to ... scheduled areas and tribal areas" and never pairs them. The pairing
is only implied by p.105 (Naga Hills-Tuensang, "tribal area ... specified in the Sixth Schedule"). Cite p.105 in `pg` and in the
explanation, or drop the swapped-schedule statement. Key unaffected ("2 only"); the same pairing is used in 102's distractors.

**M5 - CS-POL-ch06-104 (LK-6.1): Sikkim is listed in the stem as a territory India "acquired".** Author note 3 asked that the old
pack's "Sikkim labelled acquired" flaw not return. The old items are gone, but this stem repeats the label (p.94 does list
Sikkim among acquisitions, so it is supported, but pp.100-101 describe a protectorate, associate state, then state by
referendum and the 36th Amendment). Drop Sikkim from the stem (Puducherry, Goa, Daman and Diu, Dadra and Nagar Haveli
are enough); key unaffected.

**M6 - pack header `source_code` is "lk05" (top-level), "lk06" under `chapter`.** Every other pack has the same code in both
places. Fix the top-level value to "lk06" if the loader or checker uses it.
