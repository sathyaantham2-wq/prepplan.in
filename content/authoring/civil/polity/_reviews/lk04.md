## HIGH
**CS-POL-ch04-313 (LK-4.3, which) - second defensible answer.** Statement 2: "Ministers need not be members of the
legislature in the Indian parliamentary system." Key is "1 and 3 only", with statement 2 marked false because p.64 lists
"membership of the ministers in the legislature" as a feature. In fact Article 75(5) lets a non-member be a minister for
six months, so statement 2 is true in the real Constitution, and a well-prepared aspirant would pick "1, 2 and 3". The
chapter never says that a non-member cannot be a minister (the old-pack verifier flagged the same point in
verify-lk04.md, "LK-4.3 #14", and it was reintroduced here). Fix: replace statement 2 with something p.64 supports,
for example "Collective responsibility of the executive is to the executive's own head alone" or
"The parliamentary system rests on separation of powers between the legislature and the executive".

**CS-POL-ch04-610 (LK-4.6, stmt12) - II restates I.** I: critics called the Constitution a "carbon copy" of the 1935
Act. II: the critics said the framers included a large number of the 1935 Act's provisions. II is the content of the label,
not a separate reason, so "II explains I" and "II does not explain I" are both arguable (same shape as lk05 M5/418).
The key (II explains I) rests on "the ground for the label", which is a paraphrase. Fix: make II an independent fact
(for example that P.R. Deshmukh said the Constitution is the 1935 Act with only adult franchise added, then key
"correct but does not explain"), or make I a plain fact and II a real cause.
