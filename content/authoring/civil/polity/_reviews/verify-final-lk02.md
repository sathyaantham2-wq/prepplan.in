## HIGH

- **212 (LK-2.2): two defensible answers.** The key is "1 only", which makes statement 2 false: "The seats of the Chief Commissioners' provinces were allotted in proportion to population, like those of the governors' provinces."
  - p.39 point 2 says "Each province and princely state ... were to be allotted seats in proportion to their respective population. Roughly, one seat per million."
  - The Chief Commissioners' provinces are "provinces", and each got one seat. Delhi, Ajmer-Merwara, Coorg and British Baluchistan each had under or about a million people, so one seat each is consistent with "roughly one per million".
  - An aspirant can defend statement 2 as true, which makes "1 and 2 only" a second correct option. The explanation ("one seat each, therefore not proportional") is an inference, not a page statement.
  - Fix: replace statement 2 with a clearly false one, for example that the 93 princely-state seats were allotted equally to every state, or that seats were divided among two communities.
