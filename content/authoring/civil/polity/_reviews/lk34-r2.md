**M2 - 077 st2 "The jurisdiction specified in the Constitution can be curtailed by Parliament" (keyed false).** False only by the book's sentence ("cannot be curtailed both by the Parliament and the state legislature", p.512). A reader can defend it as true through Art 368, and the pack's own 114 (key: the 42nd Amendment debarred HCs from judging central laws) shows Parliament doing it. The alternative `Only two statements` is an option, so a strong candidate has a second route. Stem has no hedge. Fix: "can be curtailed by an ordinary law of Parliament" or open with "According to the chapter". 071 st3 and 072 st4 are safe (no alternative option survives).

**M3 - 094 st2 and 092 st3 (criminal appeals from magistrates to the HC).** The text follows p.515, but in real law a convicted person's appeal from an assistant sessions judge or metropolitan magistrate goes to the Court of Session (CrPC s.374(3)); the HC gets such cases mainly through state appeals against acquittal. The BNSS 2023 also removed the metropolitan-magistrate class. "In some cases" saves the key (`1, 2 and 3` in 094; `2 and 3 only` in 092), but 094 has an alternative option `1 and 3 only` for a BNSS-aware reader. Fix: add "According to the chapter" to the 094 stem and drop "metropolitan magistrate".

**M4 - two explanations misstate the chapter in the wrong-statement line.** 056: "Statement 4 is incorrect: the chapter states: he is otherwise deemed to be a judge of that high court" (the chapter says he will NOT otherwise be deemed one). 095: "Statement 4 is incorrect: the chapter states: they are governed by the Constitution alone." A learner reading these learns a false "chapter states". Fix: "the chapter says he is not otherwise deemed..." and "the chapter lists seven sources, not the Constitution alone".

**M5 - 052 st2 "determined by the President" has no chapter hedge.** Art 222(2) says Parliament by law, and until so determined the President by order. Today a law exists, so it is false, and the chapter says Parliament, so the key holds, but the stem should say "According to the chapter" to close the Art 222(2) reading.
