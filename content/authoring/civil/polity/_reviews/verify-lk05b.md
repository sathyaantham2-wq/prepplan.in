## Structural weaknesses of the old set (all concepts)
- All 120 items are single-line recall, Remember/Easy; none is a "which statements", "how many", Statement-I/II or match item as UPSC has set since 2020.
- Items 12-20 of every concept are templated reasoning or "chart" stems with absurd distractors ("taxes, wars", "Fascist", "Feudal"); items 1-11 often read "Choose the correct option ____".
- Several distractors are not plausible misconceptions (LK-5.10 #16 "Recall - Greek word"; LK-5.11 #4 "Nakara" offered as a person).
- Ideas repeat across items of a concept (e.g. LK-5.7 #7, #15, #18, #19 are one idea; LK-5.12 #12, #16, #20 are one idea).
