## The seven points asked about
(a) 717 is fixed. The stem now says "issues that Laxmikanth lists among the 14 tension areas" and each statement ends "is among the 14 listed areas". The list on pp.236-237 has items 9, 10 and 13 (electronic media, enquiry commissions against CMs, Centrally sponsored schemes). It has no delimitation. Statement 4 is false and the other three are true, so "Only three" is unambiguous.

(b) 619 is sound. Under Art 280(3) the Finance Commission recommends on four things: the sharing of tax proceeds, the principles of grants-in-aid, measures to supplement panchayat and municipal resources, and any matter the President refers. Options 2, 3 and 4 are those functions. GST rates, exemptions and floor rates with bands are the GST Council's (p.234 item (e)). Exactly one option is "not a Finance Commission matter", in law and on the page.

(c) 411 is sound. The Zonal Councils are statutory under the States Reorganisation Act 1956, not constitutional. The book lists them among the non-constitutional advisory bodies (p.229). Statement 3, "constitutional bodies", is false and no reading makes it true. Key "Only two" is right.

(d) 210 statement 3 is false as the item needs it. Art 249(2) and p.224 say the resolution lasts one year and is renewable for not more than one year at a time. "Six months at a time" is therefore false. The six-month figure belongs to the lapse of laws after the resolution ends (Art 249(3)) and after an emergency ends (Art 250(2)). Statements 1 and 2 use it correctly, so "Only two" is right. The trap is fair because it mixes up two real numbers on the same page.

(e) 213 is sound on Arts 200, 201, 213 and 360. Statement 1 is right (p.225 i). Statement 2 is right (iv). Statement 3 is false because Art 360 operates in a financial emergency, not President's rule (iii). Key "Only two" is right.

(f) The LK-15.7 and LK-15.8 text is supported. LK-15.7 says only the West Bengal demands were not accepted, and the ARC and Rajamannar outcomes are kept separate. The LK-15.8 idea and rule match pp.238-244. See LOW-1 for one loose phrase.
