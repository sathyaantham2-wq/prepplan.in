## MEDIUM
- **M1 Tribes Advisory Council falsifiers 034 (s2 "10 members") and 036 (s2 "12 members").** Fifth Schedule para 4 says "not
  more than twenty", so a council of 10 or 12 is lawful and some states do have fewer. The book says 20 and the items are
  fair against the book, but in real law "has 10 members" is not clearly false. 30 (032 s2) is safe because it exceeds the
  ceiling. Change 034 and 036 to 30 or to "more than twenty", or write "exactly 20" language into the truth statements.
  Keys do not change.
- **M2 Council size stated as universal in 048, 049 (s3), 057 (s1), and 041.** Real law (Sixth Schedule para 2) says "not more
  than thirty, of whom not more than four nominated"; Bodoland Territorial Council has 46 (40 elected, 6 nominated), and
  Dima Hasao, Tripura and the Meghalaya councils have different splits. 041, 095 and 100 say "according to the chapter";
  048, 049 and 057 do not. 057 is the sharp one: an expert who rejects s1 ("Twenty-six of its 30 members are elected")
  gets "Only two" as an alternative. Add "according to the chapter" or "in the general scheme" to the stems of 048, 049, 057.
- **M3 Near-duplicate cluster on footnote 4: 071, 077, 079, 093.** 077 s1 repeats 071 s1 verbatim, 077 s3 is 071 s2 flipped,
  077 s2 is 071 s3 with Mizoram for Tripura, and 079 Statement-II is 071 s4 verbatim, Statement-I is 077 s2 negated. Four
  items carry about four facts. Keep 071 and 079, rework 077 (for example Mizoram/Meghalaya mix of both columns) or drop it.
- **M4 Heavy repetition: President's assent to Fifth Schedule regulations.** 023, 030(3), 037(3), 038(II), 040, 083, 088(3),
  099: eight items, one fact. Also council laws need Governor's assent (062, 074, 075, 080, 088, 099) and "26 of 30 / four
  nominated" (041, 042, 048, 049, 057) and Table 42.1 (043, 044, 050, 056, 060). Not identical statements except M3 and 099,
  but the 100 are a chapter's worth of about 45 distinct facts. Cut the weakest repeats when rewriting 099 and 077.
- **M5 Near-copy of tagged PYQ UPSC-POL-2025-477.** 033 statements 1 and 2 are the PYQ's two statements nearly verbatim
  (state loses executive power; Union takes over administration on the Governor's recommendation), and 039 Statement-I
  repeats the first. As a deliberate "learn the PYQ trap" this is defensible, but it is a copy; reword 033 so it tests
  the same law through a new angle, or accept and note. 024 option 2 and 4 also echo it as distractors (fine).
  Other PYQs (2008-175, 2015-272, 2019-363, 2022-435, 2023-454, 2026-489) are not copied. 2026-489 s1 (tribal areas of the
  four states under Fifth Schedule) is covered thematically by 014/085 but not copied.
- **M6 "Fifth Schedule emphasis is not on self-rule" (013 s3, 051, 090 s2, 098 Statement-I, 082).** Text is p.595 ("totally
  different ... where the emphasis is on self-rule"). A purist could point to PESA (Panchayats in Scheduled Areas) as
  self-rule in Fifth Schedule areas. Keys are fine because the book says it, but 013 s3 and 090 s2 assert the Fifth Schedule
  negative as fact; reword as "according to the chapter the emphasis in the Sixth Schedule regions is on self-rule, unlike
  the Fifth" to remove the dispute. Five items on one idea is also a repetition issue.
- **M7 Longest-key tell.** Key is the longest option in 11 of 29 MCQs. Clearly visible in 045 (107 vs 40/38/44), 081 and 082

- **M8 Metadata: LK-42.3 rule says "A district council has 30 members, four nominated" without a hedge.** Add "(in the
  chapter's general scheme; the Bodoland Territorial Council has 46 members)". Same hedge in scope_out wording.
- **M9 scope_out item 2 asserts "Lai (Pawi)" is a later renaming.** That is not supported by the chapter and is doubtful in
