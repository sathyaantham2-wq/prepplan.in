## HIGH
**H1 - 414 (LK-1.4), "all of them Princes" is not clearly false.** The book (p.32) says the Chamber had "120 members i.e., Princes of
108 states and 12 representatives of other states". Statement 1 says it "consisted of 120 members, all of them Princes of states" and the key
("2 and 3 only") needs statement 1 to be false. The text never says the 12 were not princes (historically they were ruling princes chosen
by the smaller states), and the explanation only repeats the book's sentence, it does not show why "all Princes" fails. A well-read
student can defend "1, 2 and 3". Replace statement 1 with a clean false statement (e.g. "It was headed by the Secretary of State
for India", or "It was inaugurated in 1926").
