  "contains elaborate emergency provisions"; Statement-II says they "enable the President to meet any extraordinary
  situation ... and safeguard sovereignty, unity, integrity and security". p.69 gives II as the rationale for having

  explain I". Two defensible answers. Fix: reword S-I to "The Indian Constitution contains emergency provisions
  (Articles 352, 356 and 360)" (then II clearly explains I), or move to a plain I-correct/II-correct-independent design.
