said the Preamble states in a brief and pithy form the argument of much of that book. Key: "II explains I". The chapter does
not support that link. Footnote 13 (p.92) gives the "brief and pithy" remark as the reason he called it a **key-note**;
footnote 14 gives a different reason for quoting it ("I am all the more moved to quote it because I am proud that the
people of India should begin their independent life..."). So "both correct, II does not explain I" is equally defensible
and the key rests on the builder's own reading, not on the page. Two defensible answers. Fix: swap II for the fn 14 reason
(moved / proud of India's tradition), which makes "explains" textual, or make the pair independent and key "does not explain".
