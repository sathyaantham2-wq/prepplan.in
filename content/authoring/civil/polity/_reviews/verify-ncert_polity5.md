## Old questions: wrong, doubtful or unsupported (index = position 1..20 in the old concept)
Wrong or misleading key (3):
- CVP-5.4 #4: "Articles that provide for the ECI are 324 to 329". Article 324 alone creates the Commission; 324-329 are the electoral provisions of Part XV (Ch.81 p.813, 819).
- CVP-5.6 #1: "Casting a vote is both a constitutional right and a duty". Laxmikanth gives no support for a duty; voting is not a legal duty in India. Only the right (Art 326 franchise) is in the text.
- CVP-5.5 #10: "1967 saw the end of single-party dominated elections". Ch.79 p.804 says the Congress dominance has been DECLINING since 1967, not ended; Ch.85 p.840 shows the Congress still won the Centre in 1967 and 1971.

Conflicts with the Laxmikanth text (3):
- CVP-5.1 #6 and #7: psephos = "pebble". Ch.84 p.835 says "the piece of pottery on which certain votes were inscribed". (The classical word is in fact pebble, so the book is imprecise; the new pack avoids the etymology.)
- CVP-5.3 #10: Commission years "1952, 1963, 1973 and 2002". Laxmikanth gives the Acts as 1952, 1962, 1972 and 2002 (Ch.23 p.329, Ch.82 p.824 fn 1). Real-world commission years differ from the Act years; the item mixes the two.

Doubtful (5):
- CVP-5.3 #7: appeals on religion/caste called an "electoral offence under RPA 1951". Such appeals are corrupt practices in RPA 1951; Laxmikanth does not classify them.
- CVP-5.3 #14: "who can vote according to RPA 1950". The age and franchise rule is Article 326 (Ch.81 p.813); RPA 1950 adds registration rules.
- CVP-5.5 #11: Janata Party "first coalition government at the Centre". Ch.85 Table 85.1 lists it first, but the Janata Party was a merged party, so the label is contestable.
- CVP-5.2 #16: "framers chose the plurality system" is incomplete: Ch.23 p.330 says some members advocated PR and the Constitution uses PR (STV) for the Rajya Sabha, Councils, President and Vice-President.
- CVP-5.4 #15: "enumerators visit households" for the roll: not in Laxmikanth.

## Real-law checks made while writing (election law has moved since the book)
- Appointment of CEC and ECs: Ch.43 gives the Anoop Baranwal (2023) interim committee (PM, Leader of the Opposition, CJI). Parliament has since legislated (Chief Election Commissioner and Other Election Commissioners Act, 2023), which is not in the book; the items say what the Court "directed ... until Parliament makes a law" and never call it the present law. The 1991 Act is described as "the Act, as described by Laxmikanth"; salary parity with a Supreme Court judge is deliberately NOT tested because the 2023 Act changed it.
- Electoral Bond Scheme (2018): struck down by the Supreme Court in 2024. Items (CVP-5.6 #4 and statement in #12) test the design as printed on Ch.83 p.832 and the explanation says so. Foreign funding (2018) is not used as a falsifier because the book's wording is broader than the amended FCRA.
- Party recognition: Ch.79 gives the criteria (Symbols Order); the book's counts of national and state parties are for 1952 and 2019 and are not tested. The list of national parties has changed since 2019.
- Delimitation: the book says the freeze runs "upto year 2026"; the Article 82 proviso refers to the first census after 2026. Items use the 42nd, 84th and 87th Amendments and the 2002 Act only.
- Falsifiers verified against real law as well as the book: Rajya Sabha not subject to dissolution; Council members not in the presidential college; Article 326 covers the Lok Sabha and assemblies only; Kuldip Nayar (2006) upheld, not struck down; plurality, not majority, wins under FPTP; Art 169 Act is not an Article 368 amendment; Tamil Nadu Council Act 2010 never in force; Art 329A repealed; condonation within 15 days saves a defector; no time limit in the Tenth Schedule; Kihoto Hollohan rejected the bias argument; 91st Amendment ceiling is 15 per cent (10 per cent was only the NCRWC recommendation); liquor ban 48 hours; withdrawal gap cut from 20 to 14 days; NOTA does not unseat a winner; VVPAT slip 7 seconds; Bill to override Lily Thomas withdrawn.

## Five least-sure facts
1. Anoop Baranwal committee composition and its status: correct as the 2023 judgment's interim direction, but superseded by the 2023 Act (CJI replaced by a Union Cabinet Minister), which is outside the book.
2. Electoral bond features (Ch.83 p.832): the scheme as printed; the 2024 judgment is the only outside fact, mentioned in two explanations.
3. "Freeze up to 2026" wording (Ch.23 p.329): kept as the book says; real Article 82 wording is "first census after 2026".
4. Table 85.1 details: the Chandra Shekhar government supported from outside by Congress (I), used as a falsifier (statement 2 of CS-POL-ncp5-5-13).
5. Tenure "six years or age 65, whichever earlier" (Ch.43 p.600) is attributed to the 1991 Act as the book does; the 1991 Act has since been repealed, though I believe the tenure rule continues.
