## Scope decisions
- IN: everything on the chapter pages that states a fact; footnotes only worded "according to Laxmikanth" where classificatory (Judges-case titles, supersession details).
- OUT: the 13 interpretation doctrines (named only, no content); the count "fifteen references" (outdated in real life, so never a key or falsifier); High Court matters.
- Falsifiers avoided where the book is silent but real law could be argued: "Constitution defines misbehaviour" (PYQ 2019 stmt), basic structure doctrine as a non-listed doctrine, Art 129 vs 215 kept only as "Art 129 relates to SC only".
- Tagged PYQ ideas reused with new angles: Art 143 (own initiative vs on reference), original jurisdiction parties, contempt Act (Sanyal, civil/criminal), retired judges, impeachment procedure, NJAC, number of judges, enlargement of jurisdiction. The 98th Amendment item (2005) is not in Laxmikanth and was not used.

## Internal inconsistencies of the chapter handled
- Ch.26 text numbers its heads 1-8 but the body headings run to 9 ("7. Review Jurisdiction", "8. Constitutional Interpretation", "9. Other Powers"); no item asks for the head number.
- Article 143 list says "fifteen references" while later references exist; no item uses the count.
- Removal "on the recommendation of the Parliament" (p.416, tenure list) is looser than the Article wording; items use the removal paragraph (address of both Houses, order of the President).
