## Notes (no change of key needed)
- LK-4.12 rule text says "Dogri"; the chapter prints "Dogri (Dongri)" in the list and "Dongri" in the amendment sentence (p.81). New pack follows the book ("Dongri").
- LK-4.7: the chapter is inconsistent on Part IX: p.62 lists only IV-A, IX-A, IX-B and XIV-A as added Parts, while p.69 says the 73rd Amendment added "a new Part IX". The new pack avoids asserting which Parts were added beyond the Note on p.75.

## Structural weaknesses of the old set (all concepts)
- 130 of 140 items are one-line direct recall of an Article/Part number, labelled Remember/Easy or Understand/Easy; none is a "which statements", "how many", Statement-I/II or match item in the style UPSC used since 2020.
- Items 15-20 of every concept are templated reasoning or "Which pair/chart/table is correct?" stems with obviously absurd distractors.
- Several stems are not self-contained ("Choose the correct option. Article ____ ...").

## New pack
content/authoring/civil/polity/packs/lk04b.json: same concept codes LK-4.7 to LK-4.13, 20 questions each (5 direct, 9 which-statements, 3 how-many, 2 Statement-I/II, 1 match), recall at most 3 per concept, all facts cited to pdf pages 62-84. Statement items use real near-miss distractors (adjacent Articles/Parts, swapped bodies, original-vs-present numbers, President-vs-Governor, Fifth-vs-Sixth Schedule, 1935 Act vs Canada). Validator: 0 problems.
