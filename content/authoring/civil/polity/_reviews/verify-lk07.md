## Cautions heeded in the new pack
- Footnote/classification claims are worded "according to Laxmikanth" (e.g. no state other than Andhra Pradesh and Telangana retains a residence requirement; Article 371-D, fn 1-2 p.126).
- The chapter says the Act "prescribes five ways" of acquiring citizenship but then numbers Assam Accord and CAA 2019 provisions as 6 and 7. No item treats the number of ways as seven.
- Articles 14 and 21 (available to all persons) are not in the chapter, so no statement says what friendly aliens do or do not get under them.
- Article 35-A / J&K items are in the past tense (till 2019).

## Structural weaknesses of the old set
- All 240 items are single-line direct recall, Remember/Easy; items 15-20 of every concept are templated "Why/Which pair/Which chart" stems with absurd distractors; items 12-20 often have options that differ only by length. Several stems are not self-contained ("Choose the correct option...").
- Timeline items (LK-7.5 #20, LK-7.7 #20, LK-7.9 #20) test list order only.
