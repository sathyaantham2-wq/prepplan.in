In real law a Prime Minister needs the confidence of the House by simple majority and minority governments exist. The fact comes only from the notes (inventory A08-028 wrongly marked ok). With stmt 2 false the item has no correct option (1 and 3 only is not offered). Fix: replace stmt 2 with a Laxmikanth-backed fact (absolute majority appears only inside the Art 368 special majority) and re-key.
- Q020 (match) pairs "B. Absolute majority" with "Forming a government after general elections", same unsupported fact; and "A. Simple majority" is equally defensible against it (a confidence vote that forms a government passes by simple majority), so the pairing is not unique in real law. Fix: replace List-II item 2 with a Laxmikanth item (e.g. "Component of the Art 368 special majority") or swap in "Removal of the Vice-President" (effective, Art 67).
