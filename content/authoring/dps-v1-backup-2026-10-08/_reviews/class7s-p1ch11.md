## HIGH (1)

1. C7S-11.2, case study "At a village haat, Lata has rice ... Gopal ... Farida ..." Parts (c) and (d) rest on a
   flawed premise. Cloth and rice are divisible goods, so Lata could hand over only Rs 500 worth of cloth (or swap
   Rs 500 of rice for Rs 500 of cloth) and lose nothing. So "divisibility stops Gopal giving back the extra" and
   "the chain loses Rs 300, so the money route is better" are not forced; a second defensible answer exists
   (barter with part-quantities works). Fix: make the Rs 800 item indivisible (a stitched shawl, a sealed
   Rs 800 sack that Farida will only sell whole) and say Lata has no other goods; or drop (c)/(d)'s "loses Rs 300"
   and ask instead which two barter problems the chain meets (double coincidence, divisibility) and why money
   removes them.

## MEDIUM (7)

2. C7S-11.4, short_answer "(b) Which function of money lets the same money pass through every step of that
   chain?" Key: medium of exchange. The book places the parents-shopkeepers-workers example inside the
   "common denomination" paragraph, so a student following the page can defensibly say common denomination.
   Fix: reword to "which function ... when the shopkeeper accepts it as payment from parents" or accept both
   in the key; also check the 5-mark long answer's "money cycle" step for the same ambiguity.
3. C7S-11.5, assertion_reason (coins accepted across kingdoms / made of precious metals). "R does not explain A" is
   arguable: precious-metal value could be why a coin was trusted abroad. The book gives no link. Fix: use an R
   that is plainly irrelevant (e.g. Chalukya coins showed Varaha and a parasol) or one that clearly explains
   ("powerful rulers' coins were trusted beyond their kingdoms").
4. Claim-check frame is templated across the chapter: 11.1 Priya, 11.2 Rohit, 11.3 Kabir, 11.4 Anita, 11.5 Ila,
   11.6 Meera all follow "X says '...only/sure/cannot...'. Do you agree?" and every verdict is "No, 'only' is
   too strong". This teaches the cue, not the concept (QUESTION_STANDARD anti-template rules). Fix: make at
   least two claims partly right (agree with a limit, or agree-and-add), and vary the frame in two concepts
   (find the error in a table, compare two students' answers).
5. C7S-11.1 and 11.3 repeat the same facts across many items. 11.1: eraser-for-pencil swap is used in four
   items (SA define money, SA transaction/barter system, SA "what each needed", the long answer) plus the
   Anil/Bela/Chitra item and Arjun's Line 1 test the same "barter omits services" point. 11.3: vendor-and-old-
   clothes gain is asked in the MCQ (Mrs Reddy), SA (Mrs Rao, steel bowl), SA "explain the practice", the long
   "justify" answer and the Iyer case. 11.4: "wheat rots, money keeps" appears in the MCQ, AR, SA, Anita claim
   and the long evaluate. Fix: keep one or two per fact and replace the rest with other ideas from the
   concept (e.g. 11.3 Radha/book-club items already cover a different angle).
6. Case studies where parts (a)-(c) do not need the chapter (section 11): 11.1 Swap Day (c) is plain addition;
   11.3 Iyer (a),(b) and Pranab (a),(b) are rate arithmetic given in the stem; 11.4 Das (a),(b) and Rohit
   (a),(c) are pure arithmetic; 11.6 Mohan Lal (a),(b) likewise. Free marks, and the Social Science content in
   several is thin. Fix: where possible make an early part name the barter problem or function involved, and
   shorten the arithmetic.
7. C7S-11.2 case "Bhima ... wheat sells at Rs 100 per kg" is unrealistic (about Rs 25-35/kg), and 70 kg of wheat
   for a goat is then worth Rs 7,000, odd next to a Rs 6,000 cash offer for the same goat. Fix: use Rs 30/kg and
   rescale the cash offer and the shoe/sweater/medicine rates (or keep the ratio but say "local price").
8. C7S-11.6 case "Mohan Lal" part (d): the key's reasons (a bank deposit "leaves a record", the trip to the
   bank) are not from the chapter though the stem asks for "a reason from the chapter". Fix: ask for a reason
   using what the chapter says (difficulty of carrying/storing many notes, money going directly between bank
   accounts) and change the key to that.
