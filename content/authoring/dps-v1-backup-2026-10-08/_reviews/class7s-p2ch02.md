## MEDIUM
1. All concepts, pattern. Every case study ends "(d) Either with reasons ...". The key accepts both choices, so there is no real trade-off and nothing is graded beyond "gave a reason". Fix: make (d) turn on the earlier numbers (e.g. 2.8 Q20, 2.4 Q20, 2.2 Q19) or state a criterion (cost, time, number of people) so one answer is better supported.
2. Templating across concepts. Every concept has the same slot order: Q2/Q3 "NOT ..." MCQ, Q6 multi_statement, Q7 AR, Q14 claim-check "X says '... only/never/anybody'", Q15 "write three clues / three lines", Q17 "Justify", Q18 "Compare ... on four points", Q19/Q20 case studies. Fix: vary frames in at least 3-4 concepts (e.g. replace Q15 clues in two concepts with a "find the error in this timeline" item; swap one Compare for a "which of two claims is better supported" item).
3. Claim-checks are strawmen built on an absolute word (2.2 Q14 "only about conflict", 2.3 Q14 "no efforts at peace", 2.4 Q14 "anybody ... nobody", 2.5 Q14 "only with the highway", 2.7 Q14 "only through armies"). All eight are false by the cue word, which teaches the cue (QUESTION_STANDARD s4). Fix: make two of them true or true-for-a-limited-case (e.g. "SAARC's work has been affected by tensions" - agree).
4. 2.1 Q19 (case). (a) puts Afghanistan under "land neighbour"; 2.5 Q11 teaches that Afghanistan "once shared a direct land border" and Pakistan's creation complicated it. The book's framing text does list it as a land neighbour, so the key survives, but the bank states both. Also 2.1 Q2 explanation says Afghanistan "shares land boundaries". Fix: swap Afghanistan for Nepal/Bangladesh in the card list, or word the sorting "as in the chapter's framing passage". (b) 15,100-11,100 is pure subtraction of two numbers printed in the stem (free mark).
5. 2.1 Q20 (case). Applying the Indian Ocean's world shares to a count of "world voyages" and then to what "Mundra could handle" is not what the book says. Key (d) says "containers ... steady port traffic" (not in the book) and "either", although oil (2,400) vs container (900) clearly favours one. (b) "the bulk-cargo and container voyages" is ambiguous: 800 and 900 or 1,700. Fix: ask for each separately, drop the Mundra claim, make (d) a defined comparison.
6. 2.2 Q20 (case). (c) is answered by the stem (imports of electronics and equipment are listed), and (d) is a strawman ("stop buying altogether"). The key says the chapter "favours dialogue and trade"; the book only says efforts are being made. Fix: remove the imports sentence from the stem, and offer two real options (e.g. widen exports vs diversify suppliers) rather than a strawman.
7. 2.3 Q19 (case). (a) 2019-550 = 1469 uses only stem figures (free mark). (d) turns on visas/permits and travel to Hinglaj (Balochistan), which the chapter does not give, so no chapter-based decision exists. Fix: replace (a) with a chapter fact (the number of years Guru Nanak spent at Kartarpur, 18) and give the tour options concrete chapter-based costs.
8. 2.4 Q20 (case). (a) and (b) count trucks the stem already groups into "into Nepal"/"coming the other way" (free arithmetic). Truck counts are not trade value, so (d) is arbitrary. (c) "Which words of the chapter" is phrase-recall. Fix: add a chapter-dependent step (classify goods as India's supply vs Nepal's export) and make (d) use values, not counts.
9. 2.5 Q19 (case). (a) is in the stem (picture caption says the river is the boundary), (b) 2018-2008 is stem arithmetic, (c) is stated in the stem (agreement eased movement, uncle warns of restrictions). Three of four parts need no chapter. Fix: strip the stem to the setting and ask (a)-(c) from the chapter.
10. 2.6 Q20 (case). (a) 2010-1985 and (b) 2004-1965 use dates printed in the stem; only (c) needs the chapter. Same fix: put the dates in the chapter, not in the stem. Also 2.6 Q19 (a) 1.5-1 is stem arithmetic.
11. 2.7 Q20 (case). (c) "What does Tamil being official suggest" is answered in the stem ("Singapore offers Tamil as an official language"); (a) is the same 9 per cent x population move as 2.7 Q11 (8,000 -> 720 vs 1,20,000 -> 10,800): a near-duplicate. Fix: change Q11 to a different Singapore/Malaysia item.
12. 2.8 Q20 (case). Not a trade-off: key says Route B is "favoured by the chapter" (the chapter never compares routes), and no cost/time data is given. (c) 600/20 = 30 is correct but trivial. Fix: give time/cost for both routes or drop the decision to a justified-either with a stated criterion.
13. 2.8 Q19 (case). (a) uses the "over 10 per cent" figure printed in the stem (free). Fix: leave the percentage out of the stem.
14. 2.2 Q10, Q15, Q20 and 2.2 Q17/Q18: the "India's exports Rs X lakh crore, multiply by 8" move appears three times in one concept (Q10, Q15 "100 and 800", Q20 "1.4 -> 11.2"). Similarly 2.5 repeats Uttarapatha/Gandhara=Kandahar in Q5, Q10, Q15, Q20, and 2.3 repeats Kartarpur permit-not-visa in Q4, Q6, Q8, Q14, Q18, Q19. Fix: keep one or two per fact.
15. 2.4 Q10 stem is garbled: "Name two festivals ... giving the Indian name of the festival for Dashain and Tihar." The key (Dashain=Dashahara, Tihar=Deepavali, Holi also) cannot be marked consistently. Fix: "Dashain and Tihar are celebrated in Nepal. (a) Give the name by which each is known in India. (b) What do shared festivals strengthen?"
16. 2.7 Q7 (AR). R ("Tamil is one of Singapore's four official languages") is just the negation of A ("only three official languages"), so it tests nothing about explanation. Fix: use a different R such as a fact about Little India or the Indian-origin share, and make A about Tamil's status.
17. 2.1 Q18 asks for "one benefit of the link" on the traditional (land) side; the chapter gives none, and the key's step 4 is "a landscape or boundary fact". Fix: ask for benefit on the maritime side only.
18. 2.2 Q16 asks "where it began, when, who" but step 2 awards a mark for "routes of trade and pilgrimage", which the question does not ask. Fix: add "and how" to the question or change the step.
19. Level mix (chapter): Remember+Understand = 77/160 = 48 per cent (limit 60), Analyse/Evaluate/Create = 46/160 = 29 per cent (min 15): passes.
