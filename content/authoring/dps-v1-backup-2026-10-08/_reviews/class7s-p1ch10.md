## HIGH

1. C7S-10.6 scenario MCQ "Ravi's family holds a puja at home without seeking anybody's permission..." Keyed Secular; Liberty is a second defensible answer (Liberty = no unreasonable restriction on how citizens act on their thoughts). The key's own explanation admits Liberty is "a tempting second choice". Fix: add to the stem "the State has no official religion and treats every faith's practices with equal respect", or replace the Liberty option with Justice/Equality.

2. C7S-10.4 case study "A Gram Sabha in a Bihar village received three letters". Part (b) key says Letters 1 and 3 "involve duties and rights that can be enforced". The chapter says only Fundamental Rights can be demanded in court and Directive Principles cannot; it says nothing about Fundamental Duties being enforceable. Letter 1 (father refuses school to a 9-year-old) is also ambiguous between Fundamental Duty (f) and the child's Right to Education (Art 21-A), and part (c) then asks the Gram Sabha to fund a case on it. Fix: make Letter 1 an unambiguous duty with no court question, or change (b) to "Which letter(s) can the chapter say a judge will help with?" with key Letter 3 only, and (c) to Letter 3 versus another right-based letter.

## MEDIUM
