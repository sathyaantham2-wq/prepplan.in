The "only one" claim in idx 10(c) rests on a rule the book does not state for gases, so a careful student could also ask about (v). Fix: in idx 10 drop (v) from the list (or state "oxygen is the solute" in the stem), and in idx 18(b) give the naming in the stem or accept "oxygen dissolves in water" as the whole of (b).
