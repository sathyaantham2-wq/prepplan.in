## HIGH (second defensible answer)

1. C8SC-6.1 idx 10, part (b): "What would have to change for the blunt nail to go in just as easily?" Key says only "a much
   larger force". Equally correct, and the more natural reading, is "make its tip smaller (sharpen it)". A marker holding the key
   would fail a correct "sharpen the tip". Fix: ask "(b) With the same force, what would have to change about the nail for it to
   go in as easily? Give the change in area" or make the key accept both a larger force or a smaller tip area.
2. C8SC-6.3 idx 11 (sucker-lamp case study), part (d): "The shelf has room only for a small cup. Which sucker?" Key: S1 (120 N
   against a 100 N lamp). S3 (150 N, cup 0.003 m2) is also defensible: it holds with a 50 percent margin and "small" is undefined.
   Fix: state a limit, e.g. "the cup can be at most 0.0025 m2", so S1 is the only sucker that fits and holds, or make (d) a
   safety-margin question (lamp can swing and load rises to 140 N) with one answer.
