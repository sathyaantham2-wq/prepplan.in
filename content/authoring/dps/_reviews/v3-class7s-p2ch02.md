## HIGH
1. C7S-2.6 Q10 (assertion_reason). "A: Many Tamil families from Sri Lanka moved to south India, especially Tamil Nadu, during the civil war. R: The Tamil minority of Sri Lanka has close cultural ties to India." Key: both true and R is the correct explanation. The book (p.46) says "Because of the fighting, many Tamil families had to leave Sri Lanka and move to south India". The cause of the move stated by the book is the fighting; the cultural ties are given as a separate fact in the sentence before. So "both true, R is not the correct explanation of A" is equally defensible and the book never says the families chose Tamil Nadu because of the ties. Smallest fix: change R to the book's own causal sentence ("The families had to leave Sri Lanka because of the fighting between the Sinhalese majority and the Tamil minority") and keep the key; or keep R and change the key and answer text to "not the explanation" (but then the concept would hold two "both true, R not the explanation" ARs, see MEDIUM 5).
