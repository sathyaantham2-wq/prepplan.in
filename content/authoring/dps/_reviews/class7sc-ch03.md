## HIGH (wrong key / second correct answer / fact error)

1. C7SC-3.6 Q20 (a)-(d) case study, part (b): "Which two of the six would suit covering a wire?" Key: "the rubber eraser and the plastic scale". The stem's own data gives THREE insulators (wooden block, rubber eraser, plastic scale; and part (a)'s key counts three). Smallest fix: "(b) Which two of the six materials does the chapter name as used to cover wires?" or drop the wooden block from the cover question ("Which of the insulators in the list does the chapter name for covering wires?"), and keep wood for Mita's wire in (c).
