What percentage of the collected mass is bottle caps?" Key: 2/30 = 6.7%. Read literally, sand 30 + caps 2 + plastic 1.5 = 33.5 kg, so the answer is 2/33.5 = 6.0%. Two defensible answers. Fix: say "30 kg of beach material that includes 2 kg of caps and 1.5 kg of plastic" (then 2/30 stands), or ask for the percentage of the 33.5 kg.
2. **C6SC-9.2 [20] (case study, sooji and cashew), part (d).** The stem says the cashew bits are all "about 4 mm" and Sieve X left exactly the 80 g of cashew on top. The key then says a new 3 mm sieve is needed because "X would remove all the cashew bits, even small ones". The mixture holds no bit under 3 mm, so a 3 mm sieve gives the same result as X, and X is equally correct. The "keep the smaller bits" premise contradicts the data. Fix: give the cashew bits a spread (for example 80 g in total, 50 g about 2 mm and 30 g about 5 mm, with X at 1 mm and Y at 6 mm), so that only a new 3 mm sieve gives the wanted split. Then redo (a) to (d).

The same gap touches C6SC-9.6 [7] ("dry sand and dry salt" must need two or more methods) and the distractor in C6SC-9.2 ... see MEDIUM 4. Fix: state "sand grains and salt grains of the same size" in all three items, or change the claim to "needs the salt dissolved in water".
