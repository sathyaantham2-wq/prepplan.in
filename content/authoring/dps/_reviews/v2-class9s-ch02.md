## MEDIUM
1. 2.4#17 (long answer, delta port): stem asks for "two advantages and two risks that the chapter gives for deltas". The chapter gives one risk only (flooding). The mark scheme gives a mark for "a second risk or limit stated", which is outside the book. Fix: ask for "two advantages and the risk the chapter gives, plus one further risk of your own".
2. 2.6#18 (hill road): Plan 1 is described only with the chapter's negatives (steep, loose rock, tree clearing); Plan 2 only with positives (gentler ground, proper drainage), costs Rs 0.65 crore/km vs 0.67. The key says "either is acceptable", but the stem makes Plan 1 nearly indefensible. Strawman-leaning. Give Plan 1 a real advantage (for example a village cut off for 20 minutes longer in each medical emergency).
3. 2.5#19 (school plot beside sinkholes): same pattern; building a school for 300 children over known sinkholes to save Rs 47 lakh is not a close call, yet the key accepts both. Make the sinkhole evidence ambiguous (one old sinkhole 500 m away) or accept only B with the reason.
