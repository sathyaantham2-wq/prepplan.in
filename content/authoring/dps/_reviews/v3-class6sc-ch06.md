H1. [2.4] "Which two pieces change partners between the groupings" (copper wire, aluminium foil, cardboard, brass key, chalk). By shine: {Cu, Al, brass}, {card, chalk}. By colour: {Cu, card}, {Al, brass, chalk}. Every one of the five pieces has a different partner set in the second grouping (cardboard loses chalk and gains copper; aluminium and brass lose copper and gain chalk). The key names "copper wire and chalk"; "copper wire and cardboard" (newly together) is just as defensible. H2. [3.8] fill_blank "A new sample needs 5 strokes, so it is harder than wood but softer than ___" key "aluminium". Fix: "...and it is the next material above wood in the list: harder than wood but softer than ___ (name the nearest one)" or drop iron from the stem and keep aluminium and stone out.
