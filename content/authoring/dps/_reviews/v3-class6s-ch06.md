## HIGH (2, both second defensible answers introduced by the fix pass)

**H1. C6S-6.3 [12]** "(b) Use your answer to say which characteristic of a civilisation, other than urbanism, this building [warehouses] is evidence of, and why." Key: trade. The stem itself frames the building as one "used for collective purposes"; "some form of government and administration" (the chapter's reason: to manage a complex society and its many activities) is equally defensible for a collectively used storehouse, and a student answering it with that reasoning cannot be marked wrong. v2's original (b) (why a trading city needs it) was unambiguous. Smallest fix: restore it ("Explain why a city engaged in trade would need such a building") or ask "which characteristic is shown by the fact that the goods were stored to be transported", and accept only trade.

**H2. C6S-6.5 [20]** "(b) If that [copper] supply had been cut off, which Harappan craft would have been hit hardest, and why?" Key: bronze-making. The chapter says they "mastered the art of working copper"; copper-working itself loses its raw material directly, so "copper-working" (or metalwork generally) is at least as defensible as "making bronze". Smallest fix: "which Harappan metal, harder than copper, could not have been made at all, and why", answer bronze; or accept both and say so in the key.
