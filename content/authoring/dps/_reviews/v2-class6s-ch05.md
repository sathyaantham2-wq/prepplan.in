## HIGH (1)

1. **C6S-5.6[12]** "Suggest a name built the way one of the chapter's ancient names was built (after a river, a tree,
   a people or the land's limits) ... which ancient name yours imitates and how."
   Fact error in the question and the key. The book never says a name was built "after the land's limits". Bharata
   and Bharatavarsha come from a people ("the country of the Bharatas"). The Vishnu Purana and the Tamil poem
   DESCRIBE the land's limits; they do not name it from them. The sample answer ("imitates the Vishnu Purana's Bharata
   ... by naming the land after its limits") and the rubric line "Vishnu Purana: limits" would credit a false belief.
   It is also the book's own activity 3 ("If you were born 2,000 years ago..."), open with no checkable key.
   Fix: list only three builds the book supports (a river: Sapta Sindhava; a tree: Jambudvipa; a people: Bharatavarsha),
   drop "limits" from stem, sample and rubric, and have (b) name which of the three yours copies.

   the modern equivalents (Assam, Kutch) but never their east-west positions; the item rests on everyday map
   knowledge, outside what THIS book says. Fix: add the premise in the stem ("Kutch lies in the far west and Assam in
   the far northeast") or replace the item with one that needs only the chapter's equivalents.

   "north" is also a judgement call.
