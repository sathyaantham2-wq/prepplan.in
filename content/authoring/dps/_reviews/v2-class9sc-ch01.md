## MEDIUM
1. 1.6#10, #15, #18, #21 (rice estimates): the stems say "350 kcal in 100 g of cooked rice". 350 kcal per 100 g is the figure for uncooked rice; cooked rice is about 130 kcal. The chapter says "100 g of uncooked rice ... when cooked", which is ambiguous, but the items state a factually wrong label and then report the result in kg of "rice". The arithmetic is self-consistent and the sanity-check logic holds. Fix: say "100 g of uncooked rice provides 350 kcal".
2. 1.7#20(a): asks which expert's field each fabric fact (filtering percentage, airflow) falls under. The book assigns mathematics to "modelling airflow and filtration efficiency", but filtering particles is just as defensibly physics (particle motion, electrostatic attraction). The key hedges ("mathematics, with physics and chemistry"), so a mark scheme point with no single answer. Reword to ask about what the chapter assigns to modelling.
3. 1.4#2 and 1.4#10(Z): "the energy she spends is accounted for by the height she gains" is classified as a principle. Conservation of energy is commonly called a law in physics, and the stem does not name the idea. A student can defensibly say law. The key relies on the chapter's own label. Fix: name "conservation of energy" in the stem, as the chapter does. The same loose physics (energy spent equals height gained, ignoring heat) is a minor accuracy issue.
