## HIGH

**C8SC-13.4 [18] (short_answer, 2)** "A potted tulsi plant is left in dry soil ... leaves droop. (a) Which role of water in plants, named in the chapter, has been cut off? ... (c) Before people or rain added it, from which Earth system did the water in the soil come?"
- (a) The chapter names two roles of water in plants: water taken from the soil as an input to photosynthesis (p.217-218) and transport of nutrients from soil to leaves (p.218). Drooping leaves point to neither, so "photosynthesis input" is a second defensible answer to a key that accepts only nutrient transport.
- (c) The stem excludes rain ("before people or rain added it"), yet the key answers "hydrosphere (rain and groundwater)", which re-admits rain. Rain arrives through the atmosphere (water vapour, clouds), so "atmosphere" is also defensible.
- Smallest fix: (a) change to "Which role of water that carries things from the soil to the leaves ..." or have the stem say the plant cannot get nutrients; (c) drop "before people or rain added it" and ask "Which Earth system holds the water that refills the soil through rain, rivers and wells?" with key hydrosphere (or ask only for the atmosphere-to-hydrosphere step).
