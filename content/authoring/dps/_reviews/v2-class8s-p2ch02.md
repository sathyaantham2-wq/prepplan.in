H7 C8S-2.5 / 9 (short_answer, 2 marks) "(a) Name the three programmes the Bande Mataram daily supported and the fourth it added. (b) ... fourth was meant to counter."
The book (p 50) says the daily "added National Education to Swadeshi and boycott", that is two programmes plus one added. The stem asks for three plus a fourth, the key gives only Swadeshi, boycott and National Education. The third "supported" programme the stem demands is not in the book for the daily (Swaraj as a programme is the Calcutta Congress, p 51; the daily only prints "absolute national autonomy" in a quote). A student who follows the stem and writes "Swaraj" is marked wrong or has to guess. Fix: "Name the two programmes the Bande Mataram daily already supported and the one it added, and say what the added one was meant to counter."

H8 C8S-2.6 / 0 (case study, part c) "Count the rows in which, once Row 4 is corrected, someone other than the intended target was killed." Key 2 (Row 1 and Row 4).
Row 1 is drawn from p 54, which says only that "two British ladies ... fell victim to the blast". The book never says they were killed. Only Row 4 (umbrella bearer killed, p 57) is explicit, so 1 is defensible from this book and 2 needs outside knowledge. Fix: change the question to "harmed" (key still 2: the ladies "fell victim", the umbrella bearer killed) or state "killed" in the Row 1 text.
