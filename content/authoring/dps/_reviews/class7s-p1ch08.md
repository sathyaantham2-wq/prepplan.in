## HIGH (second defensible answer)

H1. C7S-8.4 #8, assertion-reason. "Assertion (A): All four char dham sites are located in the northern Himalayas." Keyed "A false, R true".
- In common usage "Char Dham" also means the Himalayan set (Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath), all in the north, so a well-read student can defend A as true. The book's sentence is about the four sites "deliberately located in the southern, northern, eastern and western corners".
- R ("12 jyotirlingas are shrines dedicated to Shiva") is true but has nothing to do with A, so the item tests nothing about reasoning.
- Fix: write A as "The four chār dhām sites the chapter describes are all in the north of India" and pair it with an R that bears on it (for example "The chapter says they appear to be placed in the four corners of India"), or swap in a different pair.
