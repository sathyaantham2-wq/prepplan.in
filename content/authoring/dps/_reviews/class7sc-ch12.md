# Review: DPS Class 7 Science ch12 "Earth, Moon, and the Sun" (gecu112)

File: `content/authoring/dps/class7sc/ch12.json`. Read: all 140 questions (7 concepts x 20), every one recomputed or fact-checked against `content/extracted/gecu112/pages/` (the whole chapter, including the end-of-chapter exercises and the exploratory projects). No sampling.

Overall: keys are almost all right and the facts follow the book closely. The chapter does NOT yet reach the DPS standard of section 9. It is a fixed slot template repeated seven times, with several free marks in case studies and strawman decisions. See "Templating" and "Standard".

## HIGH (wrong key, second defensible answer, fact error)

1. **C7SC-12.5 Q19 (case study, polar camp).** Stem: North and South Pole teams, "a solar experiment that needs the Sun to be in the sky for more than two continuous months", choose "1 April or 1 October". Part (d) asks "Which start date should the experiment take". The key says 1 April ("on 1 October the Sun has already set at the North Pole"). The stem never says which pole the experiment is at. The sister team is at the South Pole, where (book: "opposite behaviour") the Sun rises around 22/23 September, so 1 October is equally right for that team. Fix: say "the North Pole team's experiment" in the stem and in (d), or make (d) ask for each team ("which date for the North team, which for the South team") and mark both.
2. **C7SC-12.4 Q15 (show_impossible).** Key: "Since the Earth is tilted, one hemisphere is always tilted more towards the Sun than the other". False at the equinoxes (around 21 March and 23 September, book Fig 12.9), when neither hemisphere is tilted more. The "cannot be summer in both" conclusion survives, but the stated reason is a factual overstatement. Fix: "except around the two equinoxes, one hemisphere is tilted more towards the Sun", or drop the sentence and rely on the June/December reversal the book gives.
3. **C7SC-12.1 Q3 (mcq, rotation definition).** Key text: "...the second option is revolution". Options are shuffled at load, so "the second option" will point at a random option. Fix: "the option about motion around another object is revolution".
