## MEDIUM
1. C7S-1.2 #11 (trekker at Gaumukh): part (b) asks whether the chapter "backs or corrects" the remark "this is where the Ganga itself begins" and the key says it backs it. A student who answers "it corrects it: the book names the Bhagirathi, a tributary, as the river that begins at Gaumukh" is equally defensible from the same box, and real geography agrees with her. Second defensible answer. Fix: ask only what the box says ("Which river is named as originating at Gaumukh, and what does the box tell readers to remember about the Ganga?") and drop the verdict.
2. C7S-1.4 #19 (monsoon fails for a year): the key says taanka or kunds "would stay empty". The chapter says only that they store rainwater; water stored earlier would still be there for a while. A student who says they would work until the stored water runs out is right and loses the step mark. Fix: say "a second year with no rain after the stores are empty", or mark either answer with a reason.
3. C7S-1.5 #4 (Plant H vs Plant C): the stem says the board "needs electricity all year"; H costs Rs 100 crore more and gives power for 5 months, C is cheaper and runs 12 months. Only the global-warming harm favours H, so the decision is largely dictated (strawman). Fix: give H a reservoir or make the capacities comparable so cost and emissions truly compete.
4. C7S-1.5 #18: (b) asks for a benefit of waterfalls "which coal cannot" give and then to link it to the coal harm. Hydroelectric power (the only link to the harm) is also something coal gives; tourists and irrigation water are the benefits coal cannot give but they do not link to the harm. The stem is internally inconsistent. Fix: "Name one way hydroelectric power avoids the harm the chapter links with coal."
5. C7S-1.6 #10 (true_false, Godavari delta / Narmada estuary): verdict True is correct, but the key says the Narmada and Tapti "rise in the Western Ghats". The book says only that "most rivers here originate in the Western Ghats", and in the Peninsular Plateau section lists the Narmada and Tapti as west-flowing plateau rivers. The key states a fact the book does not say (and which is not true of the Narmada). Fix: drop "rise in the Western Ghats" and say "swift west-coast rivers form estuaries".
