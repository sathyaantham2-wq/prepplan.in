## HIGH (1)

1. **C6SC-3.2, index 11 (long_answer case study, "canteen in Jaipur ... Latika has Rs 30")**. Part (c) asks "Can she afford two carbohydrate servings and one fat serving?" and the key says "No: 6 + 15 + 10 = Rs 31". The stem prices per serving, and nothing says the two carbohydrate servings must be different items. Two servings of banana (Rs 12) plus groundnuts (Rs 10) cost Rs 22, which she can afford, so "Yes" is also defensible. Part (b) ("which two carbohydrate items are cheapest") nudges towards two different items, but (c) does not say so. Step 3 ("cheapest total Rs 31, so No") and part (d) ("what she should drop") both depend on the wrong reading. Fix: in (c) write "two different carbohydrate items and one fat serving" (or "one serving each of two different carbohydrate items"). The key then holds.
