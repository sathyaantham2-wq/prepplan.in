## MEDIUM

1. All three concepts: IDENTICAL SLOT PATTERN. Each concept is exactly 6 mcq (4 plain then 2 scenario), 2 A-R, 1 statement, 1 match, 8 short answer (5 at 2 marks, 3 at 3 marks, same order), 4 long answer (2 five-mark, 2 case studies); each totals 47 marks. Bloom mix is near-identical too. Fix: vary counts/order per concept (e.g. 1.1 more claim-check, 1.3 a second reverse, different case-study/long-answer split).

2. All three `multi_statement` items: the false statement is always Statement 2 and the key is always "1 and 3 only" (1.1, 1.2, 1.3). Violates the anti-template rule. Fix: make the false one 1 in one concept and 3 in another; key "2 and 3 only" / "1 only" / "all three".

3. Correct MCQ option is the longest in 12 of 18 MCQs (1.1: 5/6, 1.3: 5/6, 1.2: 2/6). 1.1 Q1-Q3 and both scenarios are clear cases (full sentence vs short filler wrong options). Fix: shorten the correct options or lengthen distractors to the same length.

4. 1.1 case study (Coimbatore, paper plane) and 1.1 case study (Pune bottles), 1.3 case study (Class 7B notice), 1.2 charts (Pooja): STRAWMAN DECISIONS. "Dev says copy the answer from the book", "the class wrote 'Experiment finished', should the teacher accept it?", "this is not science, so we cannot do anything", "the draft says the Sun goes around the Earth, print as drafted?", "Pooja wants chart 1 before chart 4". Each (d) is answered by the part before it. Fix: make (d) a real trade-off, e.g. two club projects (bottle count pattern vs poster) and which one the data supports; or a notice that is correct on two movements and short on one, so printing/editing is a judgement.

5. Case-study parts that do not climb: 1.1 Pune: (a) and (b) are two recall facts about one paragraph, (c) is a strawman choice. 1.2 Nagpur: (d) asks for "a stall linking heat with water" and the stem already says so; the stem also names what stall B tests (coins, spoons, lamp), so (a) is nearly given. 1.2 Ms Das: (d) restates (a). 1.3 Ranchi: (a) "what did early humans use shadows for" is implied by the pole-and-pebble set-up, and (d) "another way of telling time" is trivially "a clock". Fix: make (b)/(c) apply the idea to the data given, and (d) combine two earlier answers.

6. 1.2 and 1.1 and 1.3: HEAVY REPETITION of the same 4-5 sentences. Examples: paper plane confirmed-experiment idea appears 3 times in 1.1 (Imran MCQ, Tanvir claim-check, Coimbatore case study); "science is not just facts" appears in Statement 1, the "process" SA, "Differentiate", "Show with three points", "Besides discovery" SA and the 5-mark (a)-(c). In 1.2 the tour ORDER is tested six times (Arrange SA, "backwards" chain, "torch to glacier" trace, "first half in five lines", Hari 5-mark, Ms Das charts). The chapter is five pages of overview, so 66 questions is too many. Fix: cut about a quarter, keep one item per distinct idea, and move to different skills (e.g. pick which tour topic a given everyday observation belongs to).

7. 1.2 SA "(a) Name the four examples of change ... (b) Which of them is running out so that it cannot be used again?" Duplicates MCQ "Which example does the chapter give of something that runs out..." (same fact, same answer), and (b) is a free mark from (a)'s wording. Fix: drop one; or (b) "Which of the four would a student expect to reverse, and which not?" with a reason (the book only asserts some can and some cannot, so keep it to the battery).

8. 1.2 and 1.3: two long answers use the same frame "Write/Describe the first/second half of the chapter's tour in five short lines, one each on (a)...(e)". Five recall lines for 5 marks is recall in disguise. Fix: replace one with a genuine decision (e.g. "a new Class 7 student has 3 minutes to read: which two parts of the tour should she not skip and why") or merge into one item.

9. 1.3 "Show that the shadow method ... could not work at night": key says "At night there is no Sun, so no shadow forms". Not true as stated: lamps, a torch or the Moon form shadows (the chapter itself mentions shadow puppets and light we generate to read at night). The Ranchi case study has the same issue because floodlights do cast shadows. Fix: write "no shadow of the Sun" / "no Sun-shadow whose position moves with the Sun" in the key and in Ranchi (c), and say in Ranchi's stem that the floodlights are fixed so their shadows do not track time.

10. 1.3 SA "Differentiate between the Earth rotating on its axis and the Earth going around the Sun, as the chapter describes the two movements": the key is circular ("rotating on its axis is turning around its axis; going around the Sun is moving around the Sun") and the chapter says nothing more. Both marks are free. Fix: replace with "Which body moves around which: (a) the Moon (b) the Earth" or a claim-check.

11. 1.1 match item 2 ("A wise person is a whys person") and the 1.1 poster/answers use the saying from Activity 1.1 (page 6). scope_out: "Activity 1.1 question-making". The saying is quoted on page 6 in that activity, and the match answer "asking questions matters as much as answering" is the activity's point. Fix: replace the match pair with another page 1-5 line (e.g. "Early inventors studying bird wings" and "responsibility").

12. 1.1 SA "Using one example from the chapter's own tour, explain how a topic from physics and earth science can be connected": the chapter never labels heat flow "physics" or the water topic "earth science", and does not offer this pair as its example of interconnection. The key and a mark ("Names heat flow as the physics topic...") claim more than the book (QUESTION_STANDARD 11). Also cross-concept (heat and water belong to 1.2). Fix: ask "name two of the four fields the chapter lists" and "why can a discovery in one inspire a question in another?"; or accept any pair the student justifies.

## LOW

13. 1.1 SA "(b) Using your answer to (a), state one role the chapter says science can play": (b) does not follow from (a) (responsibility), it is a separate sentence. Reword (b) "What does the chapter say follows from this responsibility?" or drop the "using" link.

14. 1.1 SA "Differentiate between a student who treats science as facts to memorise ...": the memorising student is not in the book; the key is the author's description. Acceptable as a scenario but marks should accept any sensible answer. Step names are generic.

15. 1.2 AR #2: Assertion "Water that evaporates from the seas may trickle underground far away" is loose: the book says it evaporates, falls as rain, and the rain may trickle down. Reword A: "Rain that falls may trickle underground far from the sea."

16. 1.2 AR #1: R "Ice melts into water" is a true but trivial R (the intended "true but not explanation"). Acceptable; a better R would be "Some changes can be reversed".

17. 1.2 reverse item: stem says the tour "ends" the chain with metals/non-metals, but in the book classification is the second step, not the end. Reword "the tour reaches ... after asking". Also the tag `reverse` does not match the DPS sense (write a situation); it is a reordering item.

18. 1.2 and 1.3 distractor quality: 1.3 MCQ1 ("Growing, glowing and melting"), MCQ4 ("do they cast eclipses?", "How do plants circulate blood?") and MCQ2 ("Lamps run on batteries") are invented or cross-topic throwaways, not real same-chapter confusions. Best distractors in the file are 1.3 scenario "limited to things around us at home" and "useful only for shadow puppets" (flipped book lines). Use more of that style. Match distractors in 1.1/1.2 keep three pairs right in every option (just swapping two), so partial knowledge eliminates them.

19. 1.2 Hari 5-mark: "water cycle" is not a phrase in the chapter, which only says water evaporates, falls as rain and may trickle underground. Use "the water topic". The assertion "belongs in a book about life processes" is also a mild strawman.

20. 1.3 Rhea claim-check: key third point ("the Moon's movement is tied by the chapter to shadows and eclipses") is the author's inference, and the key paragraph is muddled. Simplify: verdict, "day and night depend on receiving light from the Sun", and "the Moon going around the Earth is one of the movements the chapter lists for eclipses". 

21. 1.3 SA "Why 'more important'": the stem gives "light helps us see", so the first mark is free.
