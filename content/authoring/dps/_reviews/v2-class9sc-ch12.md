## MEDIUM
1. 12.5 L20 (science-fair exhibits) (a)/(d). Part (a) key "Moss and fern" while Spirogyra lives in and reproduces in water; a student writing "Spirogyra, moss and fern" is defensible. Part (d) ranks Spirogyra last for coping with three days without water supply, but it sits in a tank that already holds its water, so it is arguably the best coper. Second defensible answer; fix the premise (e.g. say the tank is drained) or drop Spirogyra from the ranking.
2. 12.3 L18 (ii) "Which organism's placement became easier when Protista was added?" key: Amoeba only. The chapter's own confusion sentence names Amoeba, Paramecium AND bacteria, so the bacterium is a second defensible answer. Reword to "which of the five that moves and is single-celled" or accept both.
3. 12.5 L19 (lichen cover at three sites). The book says lichens change colour with air pollutants; it never says lichen cover falls where air is polluted. Parts (a)-(b) rest on that unstated link, and forest edge vs highway also differ in moisture and bark (confounded). Restate the data as colour change, or add the link to the stem.
4. 12.1 L19 (b) key says "Yes: 24% endemic is a large number". The book gives no threshold for "large number", so "No/cannot say" is defensible. State the book's criterion in the stem (e.g. "treat 20% or more as a large number") or accept a reasoned either way.
5. 12.2 L20 (c) "Which benefit of classification made it possible to study this species separately from the other three hornbills?" key: identifying and naming. Benefit 4 is about newly discovered organisms; benefit 2 (understanding similarities and differences) fits better. Two defensible answers.
6. 12.1 L17 stem "Four fields of 2.5 hectares each give 30 quintals" can be read as 30 q in total. Write "Each field of 2.5 hectares gives 30 quintals".
7. 12.6 L17 gives every fact (which phyla, which skeleton) in the stem, so (i)-(iv) are counting and a percentage with no thinking; easy in disguise for a 5-mark Analyse item. Ask the student to derive the groups from described animals instead.
