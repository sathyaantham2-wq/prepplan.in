## HIGH

1. C9SC-9.3 idx 15 (show_impossible, "hydrogen cannot form a double bond"): the model answer and the second step mark are wrong. A double bond means two shared pairs. Hydrogen would then have 4 electrons around it, not "1 + 2 = 3". The model answer also says hydrogen shares "two of its own electrons", but hydrogen has only one electron. Fix: H has one electron and can share only one; a double bond would put 4 electrons in a shell that holds 2, so only a single bond is possible. Step 2 should say "4 electrons, above the capacity of 2".
