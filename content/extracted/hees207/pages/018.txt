The city promotes mixed-use spaces where homes, offices,

and recreational spaces like parks and museums exist nearby.

Marina Bay Sands and Raffles Place (Fig. 7.24 and Fig. 7.25) are

just a few kilometres apart.

The city’s mass public transport system connects rail, buses,

cycling paths, and green walking corridors for pedestrians

that reduce reliance on cars, encourage walking and help

create vibrant public places. To utilise the limited available

land, Singapore developed sustainable high-rise buildings with

rooftop and vertical gardens, earning the title of a ‘garden city’.

This is not only aesthetically pleasing but also reduces heat

during summers and supports biodiversity.
