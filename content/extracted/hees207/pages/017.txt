Dynamic urban planning of Singapore

Singapore, a small island nation with over 8,000 people per

sq. km, is often seen as a model of urban planning. Despite its

density, it has managed to grow while maintaining a high quality

of life for the residents. The city has a Concept Plan for how the

land must be used that looks up to 40 years ahead. The Master

Plan is updated every 5 years to reflect the changing needs of the

city’s infrastructure, making the planning very flexible. From

an overcrowded port in the 1960s, Singapore has transformed

into a modern city-state with strong infrastructure in transport,

electricity, water supply, and waste management.
