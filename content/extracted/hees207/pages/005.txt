Economic Life Around Us
7 – India’s Urban Landscape

Æ As a small group project, make a list of adults from different

generations in and around your neighbourhood. Draft a

questionnaire to enquire into their observations about

their own village, town or city and how it has changed over

the decades. What, in their view, are the key reasons that

towns and cities have changed? If they have grown larger,

what are the reasons for this? What do they miss about the

‘old days’ and what do they appreciate about the current

time? Report on your project through a presentation to

the class.
