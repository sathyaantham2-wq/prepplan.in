Exploring Society: India and Beyond | Grade 8 Part 1

The cultural environment of Thanjavur was multilingual, with

a mix of many influences. The local Tamil culture, the Telugu

culture of the former rulers, and the current rulers’ Marathi

culture interacted with each other freely.

The Maratha legacy

The Maratha rule challenged Mughal dominance and

established the largest Indian empire before the British took

over the Subcontinent, controlling much of central and northern

India. They set up a new way of governing with an efficient

administration, and also revived the local Hindu traditions

without religious discrimination. Their brave fight against

oppressive rule and foreign power was driven by the fiery ideal

of Swarājya. Later it inspired many Indians to believe they

could govern themselves, planting the early seeds for India’s

freedom movement.

Before we move on …

Æ Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj founded the Maratha kingdom

in the 17th century. Its decades-long resistance to Mughal

power, and the experience gained from this, helped in its

pan-Indian expansion in the 18th century.

Æ The British captured India effectively from the Marathas

more than from any other Indian power.

Æ Forts were the core of Maratha state; the Marathas controlled

hundreds of forts, thus strategically strengthening their

control over the region.

Æ Their formidable navy resisted European naval supremacy

for quite some time despite lack of access to the latest

technology of the time.

Æ The Marathas inspired a new cultural confidence amongst

Indians in various regions, thus contributing to the cultural

revival and innovation.
