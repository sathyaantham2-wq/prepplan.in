3 – The Rise of the Marathas

DON’T MISS OUT

In 1665, four ships from Chhatrapati Shivaji’s fleet reached

Muscat, the capital of Oman. The ruler there captured them

and arrested the men onboard. He also prepared a few ships to

attack Chhatrapati Shivaji’s coastal possessions. But, on hearing

that Chhatrapati Shivaji’s Navy contained more than a hundred

ships, he retreated inside the harbour with his ships.

Judicial system

The Marathas had an efficient judicial system, remarkable

for its moderation in using capital punishment (the death

penalty). The panchāyat (not to be confused with the current-

day Panchayat, a governmental body) was a local gathering of

officials and prominent men, and the main body that delivered

justice. An appeal could be made to a Maratha chief in case of
