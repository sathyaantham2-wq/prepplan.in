From the Amazon Forests we travel to the Atacama Desert

in Chile (see Fig. 1.25), one of the driest places on Earth,

stretching about 1,000 km. The temperature varies from

40°C during the day to 5°C at night.

LET’S EXPLORE

The Atacama Desert is famous for stargazing, offering

some of the clearest night skies, and its rocky, Mars-like

landscape has even been used by NASA to test rovers.

However, it has also become one of the world’s fastest-

growing dumps for discarded clothes, caused by the

rise of cheap fast fashion. The UN has called this waste

problem ‘an environmental and social emergency’.
