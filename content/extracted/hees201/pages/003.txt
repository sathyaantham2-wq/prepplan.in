India and the World: Land and the People
1 – World Geography: Some Glimpses

Fig. 1.2. (1) The Mercator Projection — Preserves shape and

direction, making it useful for navigation. However, it greatly

distorts size near the poles (e.g., Greenland looks as big as Africa).

Fig. 1.2. (2) Robinson Projection — A compromise map that balances size and

shape distortions, giving a more visually pleasing ‘realistic’ world map.

DON’T MISS OUT

Look at the size of Greenland compared to India in both the

maps. In the Mercator Projection Greenland looks larger than

India whereas in fact it is smaller.
