The savannah is a tropical grassland ecosystem that has wide

open spaces, scattered trees, and seasonal rainfall. It lies mainly

between tropical rainforests and deserts, the most famous

one being in Africa. The savannah supports a rich diversity of

wildlife, including elephants, lions, zebras, and giraffes, which

depend on its grasses and waterholes. Its climate features a

distinct wet and dry season, influencing plant growth and animal

migration patterns. However, human activities such as farming,

deforestation, and climate change are threatening the balance

of these vital ecosystems.
