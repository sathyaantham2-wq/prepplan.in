India and the World: Land and the People
1 – World Geography: Some Glimpses

Before we move on …

Æ The Earth’s surface includes varied landforms and

water bodies such as mountains, plateaus, plains,

deserts, grasslands, rivers, and oceans, all shaped by

natural processes like tectonic movements, erosion, and

deposition.

Æ These features influence climate, ecosystems, and human

activities such as settlement, agriculture, and trade.

Æ Human actions like deforestation, overuse of resources,

and pollution disturb this balance, causing issues like

desertification, biodiversity loss, and climate change.

Æ Studying world geography helps us understand these

features and the need to manage them wisely for

sustainable development.
