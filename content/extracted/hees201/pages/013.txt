1 – World Geography: Some Glimpses

Gobi Desert in the ‘Land of the Eternal Blue Sky’

Deserts are large, extremely dry areas of land with low

precipitation (less than 25 cm per year). Deserts are found on

every continent and cover about one-fifth of the Earth’s land area.

According to the National Geographic Society, around one billion

people (one-eighth of the world’s population) live in deserts.

As we move to the northeast, we arrive at the Gobi Desert in

Mongolia (part of the desert is in north China). Mongolia is

called the ‘Land of the Eternal Blue Sky’ because it has about 8

to 9 months of sunny weather, but winter temperatures can drop

to minus 30°C. The climate is harsh and vegetation sparse; herds

migrate seasonally to access pastures supported by underground

water supplies.
