India’s Demographic Dividend —

A Limited Window of Opportunity

The population pyramid for 2020 in Fig 6.16 shows that a large

portion of the population comprises children and young adults.

This phenomenon of a large young population (16–30 years) is

known as the youth bulge, which is common in countries at

Stage 2 of demographic transition shown in Fig 6.12.

Around 27 per cent of the Indian population is under the age

of 15 years and nearly 65 per cent is in the working-age group

(15–59 years).
