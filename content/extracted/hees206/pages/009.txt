6 – Dynamics of Population

Many factors have contributed to falling fertility and birth

rates. One important factor is improvements in access to quality

education, especially among girls. As women are increasingly

getting formal education and are working in formal jobs,

they are more likely to marry at a later stage and have fewer

children. Also, with rapid urbanisation, the cost of living is high

particularly in cities, so people prefer to have smaller families.
