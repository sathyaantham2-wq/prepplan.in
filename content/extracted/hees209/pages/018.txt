Nunes, who visited the Vijayanagar Empire: “He [the King] has

also women who wrestle, and others who are astrologers and

expenses that are incurred inside the gates, and others whose

duty it is to write all the affairs of the kingdom and compare

their books with those of the writers outside; he has women
