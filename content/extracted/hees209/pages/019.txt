Women engaged with the royal family were educated and

initiative of women, all for public use.

Education and Knowledge Traditions

Invasions and military campaigns during this period disrupted

India’s educational landscape as well. Yet, from villages to

temples, the tradition of learning persisted, and was safeguarded

and nurtured by kings, saints, poets, and scholars, often against

great odds.

Institutions of learning
