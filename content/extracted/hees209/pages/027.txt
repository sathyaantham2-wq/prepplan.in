Unani medicine, introduced from West Asia, was often followed. In Tamil

Nadu, preference went to the Siddha system of medicine (which shares

Ayurveda’s theoretical foundations). Many healing methods relying on plants

and minerals were passed on orally or recorded in palm-leaf manuscripts.

disruptions from the political context, the development of

science and technology remained integral to Indian society.
