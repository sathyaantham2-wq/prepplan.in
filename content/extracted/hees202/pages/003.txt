Independence

Disobedience

Movement begins

with the Salt March

Singh,

Rajguru, and

Sukhdev

executed;

Gandhi–Irwin

Pact signed

Purna Swaraj at

the Lahore session

of the Congress

1940: Lahore

Resolution,

led by

Muhammad

Ali Jinnah,

demands

separate

Muslim-

majority

states

1927–28: Simon Commission boycotted; Lala Lajpat Rai

dies after police lathi charge; the Motilal Nehru Report

calls for dominion status

1919–1922:

Khilafat

Movement

1916: Home Rule

movement by Tilak and

Annie Besant; Lucknow

Pact promotes Congress–

League cooperation

1907–1914:

M.K. Gandhi

launches the

Satyagraha

movement

of Indians in

South Africa

against racial

discrimination.

1915: M.K.

Gandhi

returns to

India from

South Africa

1917–18: Start

of satyagraha

movements

1919: Rowlatt Act;

Jallianwala Bagh

massacre; Lala Lajpat

Rai leads protests

1944: INA campaigns in

northeast India are defeated

1942: Cripps Mission proposes

post-war dominion status; rejected

by Indian leaders; Quit India

Movement launched; mass arrests

and repression

1946: INA trials,

Royal Indian

Navy Mutiny, and

Cabinet Mission

Plan weaken

British authority

1946: Direct

Action Day,

called by Jinnah

and the Muslim

League, triggers

widespread

communal

violence

1943: Indian National Army

reorganised under Bose;

provisional government of

Azad Hind proclaimed

1941: Subhas

Chandra

Bose escapes

from India to

seek support

from the Axis

powers against

British rule

cooperation movement
