 Write a news report based on an important event in the

Freedom Movement (Swadeshi, boycott, satyagraha,

Non-Cooperation, Quit India, or Partition).

a) Write a headline.

b) Include one interview or public reaction.

c) Explain why this event was important.

 Imagine you are part of a team creating a small museum

exhibition on India’s freedom struggle for students of

your age.

a) Choose a title for your exhibit.

b) Select three objects (for example: a letter, a

photograph, a newspaper, a prison diary — you can

add to these examples) and explain what each object

represents.

c) Write a short note on what visitors should learn or

feel after seeing this exhibit.
