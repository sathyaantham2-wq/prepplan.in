The Quit India Movement

In the thick of World War II, Churchill, under pressure to seek

India’s support, sent Stafford Cripps to India with an offer. The

‘Cripps Mission’ of 1942 promised to grant India dominion

status once the war was over, but with a provision that provinces

could opt out of the post-war India, if they so wished. A few

Congress leaders, like C. Rajagopalachari, favoured acceptance
