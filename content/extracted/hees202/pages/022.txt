When the judge asked Tilak if he had anything to say after being

sentenced, Tilak replied, “There are higher powers that rule the

destinies of men and nations; and I think, it may be the will of

Providence that the cause I represent may be benefited more by

my suffering than by my pen and tongue.”

What message do you think he was trying to convey?

birthright and I shall have it.” What does this reveal about Tilak’s

character?
