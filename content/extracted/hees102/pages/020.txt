Exploring Society: India and Beyond | Grade 8 Part 1

As I grew in knowledge,

I was overwhelmed with

shame. Not being a Muslim

myself, it was unmeet [i.e.,

inappropriate] to force

others to become such.

What constancy is to be

expected from proselytes

[i.e., converted people] on

compulsion?”

His long reign lasted

almost 50 years (1556 to

his death in 1605); while

its middle period was

relatively peaceful, the

final 15 years involved

fresh military campaigns

in Kashmir, Sindh, the

Deccan, and Afghanistan.
