Krishnadevaraya

In the 16th century, the Vijayanagara Empire reached its peak

under its celebrated ruler, Krishnadevaraya, who expanded

and secured the empire’s dominance over the Deccan. Under

his rule, the empire achieved both military power and cultural

renaissance. He patronised poets and scholars in Sanskrit,

Telugu and Kannada; he himself composed an epic poem in

Telugu, Āmuktamālyada, on the story of the Tamil poet-saint

Āṇḍāl; one section of the work is a Rājanīti (‘royal policy’) where

he expounded his ideas of good governance. Krishnadevaraya

provided grants to many temples, including Tirupati in Andhra

Pradesh and the Vitthala temple in his own capital Vijayanagara,

which displayed many grand temples, palaces and other

buildings.
