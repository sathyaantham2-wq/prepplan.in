LET’S EXPLORE

A builder planned to start

constructing a complex

of flats in 2011. A family

decided to buy a flat, paying

an advance of almost

₹65 lakhs. The family visited

the site regularly and found

that even after four years,

there was no sign of a building coming up where their flat

was supposed to be constructed. This was a breach of the

agreement with the builder. So, the family went to the National

Consumer Commission to complain about the builder. After

listening to both parties, the Commission ordered the builder

to return the amount to the family with interest, and also to

pay the cost of the litigation. (The names of the concerned

parties are not mentioned to protect their privacy.)
