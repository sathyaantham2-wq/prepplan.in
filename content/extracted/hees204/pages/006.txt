The Judicial System in India

The Indian Judicial system is a unified system, comprising of a

hierarchy of courts with the Supreme Court of India at the apex

level, the High Courts at the State level and Subordinate Courts

at the District level.

The Supreme Court of India

The Supreme Court of India is the highest court of law in the

country, which came into existence on the date when the

Constitution of India came into effect, i.e., 26.01.1950.

The Supreme Court is the final interpreter of the Constitution of

India.

Article 124 of the Constitution provides for the establishment of

the Supreme Court of India, which is seated in New Delhi.
