DON’T MISS OUT

In the 1970s, the effect of air pollution on the monument began

to be noticed. It was caused by local industries using coal and

other fossil fuels, which left a yellowish tinge on the marble.

Following the recommendations of an expert committee and a

PIL in the Supreme Court, a ban was imposed in the mid-1990s

to prevent industrial pollution in the area.
