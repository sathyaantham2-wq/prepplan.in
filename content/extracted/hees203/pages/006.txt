We visited the Indus/Harappan/

Indus-Sarasvati civilisation in

Grade 6 (chapter ‘The Beginnings

of Indian Civilisation’). Harappan

architecture represents the

earliest known tradition of

urban planning and construction

in the Indian subcontinent. It

is characterised by carefully

laid-out cities, standardised urban

concepts, and an emphasis on

drainage, water management,

and civic organisation. Most

Harappan cities were constructed

with bricks, as they were located

in alluvial plains where clay is

abundant.
