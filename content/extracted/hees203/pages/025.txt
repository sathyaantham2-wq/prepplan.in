Forts and Palaces

Fig. 3.33. A general view of the Mehrangarh Fort, with the Jaswant Thada in the foreground.

As we already saw in a few earlier chapters of ‘Tapestry of the

Past’, forts played an important role in Indian history—that

of protecting a kingdom from the onslaught of invaders. They

were therefore strategically located near important land or sea

routes, and often atop a hill so as to command a view of the plains

below. Forts also accommodated the residences of the ruling

and military classes, and in some cases even an entire city,

depending on their size. Since some forts also sheltered a royal

palace, we have clubbed these two categories together.
