‘going around in a clockwise direction’) will

LET’S EXPLORE

Æ Form groups in the class to discuss the following:

Imagine you have only hammers and iron chisels. How will

you plan the carving of such a huge cave? What challenges

might the hillside offer? And once you reach the nave

(Fig. 3.13), how will you manage to carve arched beams into

its ceiling?

Æ In classical temples, what Indian words would we use

instead of ‘nave’?
