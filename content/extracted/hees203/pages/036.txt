Churches in India

Fig. 3.48. The iconic St. Philomena’s Cathedral in Mysuru was built in the

Gothic style during the colonial period, with the then Maharaja of Mysore,

Krishnarajendra Wadiyar, laying the foundation stone.

As we saw in the chapter ‘India, a Home to Many’, some Christian

communities have been living in India for many centuries.

According to tradition, the first churches were built in the

coastal areas of Kerala. Over time, different types of churches

were built, sometimes imitating classic European styles

(Fig. 3.48), at other times (especially in Kerala) incorporating

local architectural traditions (Figs. 3.49 to 3.51).
