Model Code of Conduct (MCC)

The code is followed to ensure free and fair elections, listing the

dos and don’ts for leaders, political parties, and candidates that

must be adhered to.

A few elements of the code:

i. There are rules that the party in government must follow;

this ensures that the resources available to the government

are not used for the purpose of the elections. For example,

no new schemes and projects are to be announced that may

influence voters.

ii. All candidates are expected to exercise discretion and

restraint to ensure that the elections take place peacefully.

iii. Influencing voters through gifts in return for votes is

punishable.
