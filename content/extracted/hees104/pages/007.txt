The Dutch: commerce and competition

The Dutch arrived in India in the early 17th century and, unlike

the Portuguese, focused primarily on commercial dominance,

particularly in the spice trade. They established a Dutch East

India Company, with trading posts in various parts of India,

including, on the west coast, Surat, Bharuch, Cochin (Kochi), and

on the east coast Nagapattinam and Masulipatnam (present-

day Machilipatnam). Their most significant presence was in the

Malabar region of Kerala, where they displaced the Portuguese

from several trading centres.
