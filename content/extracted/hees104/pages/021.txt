Reshaping economic structures to serve imperial

needs

The British transformed India’s economy from a self-sufficient

agricultural system supplemented by craft and manufacture

production into a supplier of raw materials for British industry

and a market forced to buy British goods. Without going into

the technical details of this economic transformation, let us take

the example of the construction of India’s vast railway network,

often cited as a colonial blessing. While the railways did bring

people closer together and integrated India’s internal market, it

was designed primarily to move raw materials from the interior

to ports for export and to distribute British manufactured goods

throughout India. Railway routes largely ignored existing trade
