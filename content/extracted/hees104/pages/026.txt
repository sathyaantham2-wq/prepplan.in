The Great Rebellion of 1857

The British called it the ‘Sepoy Mutiny’. ‘Sepoys’, let us recall,

were the Indian soldiers enrolled in the East India company’s

British Army; its officers, of course, were almost all British.

After India’s Independence, historians rejected the term ‘Sepoy

Mutiny’, proposing a few others instead; following many

scholars, we will call the event the ‘Great Rebellion of 1857’. But
