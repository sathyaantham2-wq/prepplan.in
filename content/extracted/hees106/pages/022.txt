Yet, our democracy grows stronger when citizens stay informed

and engaged. By asking questions, sharing their views, and

participating in public discussions, people help shape better

life, and technology is making it easier to stay connected and

participation will play a key role in strengthening our Parliament

and the assembly of the state you belong to, ensuring that they
