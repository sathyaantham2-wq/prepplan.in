Governance and Democracy
6 – The Parliamentary System: Legislature and Executive

Before we move on...

Æ India’s Parliamentary system ensures that power is shared,

decisions are debated, and leaders are held accountable.

Æ The Legislature makes laws, the Executive implements

them, and the Judiciary ensures that they follow the

Constitution.

Æ The Parliament represents the voice of the people and

reflects the federal spirit of our democracy.

Æ Both at the Centre and in the states, structures are

designed to balance representation, responsibility, and

unity.

Æ Checks and balances between institutions help protect

citizens’ rights and uphold democratic values.

Æ The functioning of the Parliament needs to become more

efficient and productive so that the country’s progress is

not thwarted.

Questions and activities

1. Find out how many representatives from your state are in

each House of the Parliament.

2. What makes the Indian Parliament the “voice of the

people”? How does it ensure that different opinions are

heard?

3. Why do you think the Constitution made the Executive

responsible to the Legislature?

4. Why do you think we have chosen the system of bicameral

legislature at the Union level?

5. Try to track the journey of a recent bill passed by the

Parliament. Identify in which House it was introduced.

Were there any major debates or disagreements? How long

did it take for the bill to become a law? Use newspaper

archives, government websites, and Lok Sabha debates, or

ask your teacher for help.
