Governance and Democracy6 – The Parliamentary System: Legislature a n d Executive

The Parliament and the State Assemblies have been envisioned

as spaces for thoughtful debates and discussions where laws are

made in the interest of the people. However, when debates are

disrupted, sessions are cut short and some bills take years to be

discussed and passed, it becomes a matter of concern.

Concerns have been expressed by sections of the society about

the fact that a substantial proportion of their representatives in

the Lok Sabha have criminal cases against them, and that many

sessions are marked by angry or biased debates that do not

seriously address issues affecting the people.

The media also plays an important role in communicating the

concerns of the electorate. Fig. 6.13 expresses them with humour,

a practice common to all healthy democracies.
