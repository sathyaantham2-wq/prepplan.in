6 – The Parliamentary System: Legislature and Executive

From bill to act – the process of lawmaking

in the Parliament

Reading: The origins are in the British Parliament. Many MPs

were illiterate, so a clerk would read out the contents of the

bill for the MPs to discuss. Today MPs read the bill before the

discussions take place.

Clause: These are parts of a bill that explain the specific details

of the bill; eg. RTE defines the age group to which it applies (6

to 14 years).

Gazette: This is an official government publication that

announces legal documents and official information.
